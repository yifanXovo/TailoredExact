# Frozen current-call numerical policy

The ordinary reader qualifies the unchanged R108 returned-bound and complete
cover obligations, with R111 signed-gap/materiality rules. A contradiction is
a HOLD, never a clamp, a rounded repair, an imported own incumbent, or a silent
certificate. Qualification produced no damaged claim. Its passing records do
not establish that all future native calls will be numerically correct.

For a concrete damaged current call, use the R110/R111 conditional adjudication
policy and existing exact-row/floor/cover primitives. Bind the current input,
production PE/DLL, argv, call, actual saved matrix/domain, raw journal/return,
original time records and candidate mathematical vector. Check every actual
column bound/type and every matrix row with exact Fraction arithmetic. The
vector must be in this call's actual true-G and cutoff domain. An outside-domain
zero fleet cannot contradict a positive-G call. A cross-arm or historical
vector is mathematical data only; it is never that arm's UB, Start or cache.
The complete objective upper must contradict an actual lower claim by more
than the original 1e-7 policy, and an independent reviewer must sign the exact
current proof and byte bindings before the sidecar is activated.

Reject all lower claims from that call, including final native attributes and
necessarily dependent closures. Preserve its raw flags, nulls and errors.
Cold P and P-S retain only their own legal fleets; their independent original
nonnegative objective floor is still valid. P-S keeps its own verified H even
when native produces no incumbent. For ENS/M-B, separately rebuild the full
current cover without the damaged claim/dependent closure; preserve independent
qualified LP bounds and unaffected call evidence. A scoped proof without a
complete current cover reconstruction stays HOLD. Old hardcoded sidecars are
not reusable proofs for new call keys. Unknown or incomplete proofs remain
unqualified and cannot produce a TIE or a positive backend conclusion.

All mathematical repair is offline engineering. Do not change the measured
PE, performance helpers, tolerances, cap or comparison rules, and do not rerun
a formal arm. If its whole receipt was not saved before a reader guard failed,
retain exact time=null. Its native-end lower receipt and the actual wrapper
exit outward upper minus all preceding complete arms may establish an outward
interval. Require the entire interval within cap and an invariant comparison
over that interval, using the unchanged R111 interval comparator. Later proof
and review time is not placed inside the old performance window. A real
execution/identity/resource fault or an unresolved proof blocks the dependent
fixed task; it does not justify selecting a subset or silently replacing a role.

`adjudicate_round112.py` is a pure current-call verification entry point. It
requires current independent signatures and complete raw/vector bindings; no
sidecar currently exists. The ordinary reader deliberately refuses unresolved
damage. A necessary future reader projection must be separately reviewed
against this frozen policy and the actual affected raw, never against an old
performance tuple. No native work is authorized by this offline mechanism.

The current entry requires an existing raw audit.json. A guard failure before
that file was written remains HOLD; extending that path requires a separately
reviewed actual raw reconstruction. Scoped signatures bind the same current
key, production/campaign/model/policy/call and exact domain as the rejection
audit. Actual Y/D true G and cutoff membership are checked in addition to all
matrix rows, with exact Fraction membership and no enlarged domain tolerance.
The 1e-7 contradiction/closure threshold does not admit an outside-domain zero
vector into a positive-G call. None of these conditional paths was exercised
by qualification.
