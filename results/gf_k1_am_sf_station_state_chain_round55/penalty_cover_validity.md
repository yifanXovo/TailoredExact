# Multiple-choice penalty-cover validity

Let \(C\) contain at most one state from each station and satisfy

\[
\sum_{(i,y)\in C} c_{iy}>B_I.
\]

If every selector in \(C\) were one, the penalty budget would be violated.  Hence every integer-feasible solution satisfies

\[
\sum_{(i,y)\in C}s_{iy}\le |C|-1.
\]

Removing a member while the remaining costs still exceed \(B_I\) preserves validity and produces an inclusion-minimal cover.  The separator accepts only strict covers, enforces at most one selected state per station, canonicalizes station/state identities, and rejects duplicates and supersets of previously accepted minimal covers.
