## The physical completion question and its boundary

The [research section](../../docs/round162_physical_completion.md) gives Q,
the endpoint theorem, integer preservation, finite projection and implementation.
Q comprises exactly the registered bounded physical columns and every complete
original row whose nonzero support lies in those columns. It is a continuous
physical subsystem, not an elimination of all objective auxiliaries or an
integer fleet hull. G, state_g, zprod, ratios, e and other old auxiliaries,
their rows, types and objective remain in the outer OLD+STOCK model.
The exclusion of e defines this object; e already has a finite native bound.

Two shared scaled endpoints use the same Q, lambda_L+lambda_U=1,
G=L lambda_L+U lambda_U, X=xi_L+xi_U and perspective copies of all Q rows
and finite bounds. Existing state_g and zprod link to the corresponding
moments. All new columns are continuous with zero objective. At L=U the
implementation uses direct product identities. At an integer old solution,
including a nonminimal G epigraph, V=GX extends the point and preserves J.
Endpoint points can have fractional arcs, owners and selectors; their true
Gini is not required to equal L or U.

The lifted total-inventory bounds imply the four STOCK inequalities. Those
four rows remain explicitly present in both direct comparison arms. If an
optimal old LP point already has all state_g=G*state and zprod=G*Y, the
identical-X endpoint completion attains the same J. Current VD-P forces this
consistency at G=L or G=U, including G=L=0. Therefore this conditioning cannot
remove all of R133's zero-G/integer-objective Jensen gap.

common physical completion; the bounded endpoint hull is valid and integer
preserving but has a product-consistency no-gain boundary; only NB35 gains free
original J here; one compensated row retains most of that fixed-scope gain;

and this frozen continuous-Q conditioning candidate should stop at this layer.

finding. Do not add a parameter grid, another denominator envelope or a renamed

held-out confirmation against both E+ and STOCK.
