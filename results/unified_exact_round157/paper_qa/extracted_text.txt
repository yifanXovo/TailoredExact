    Inventory Objective Certificates and Physical Fleet Realization
                 A research section accompanying the stable GI-TBC method

where G = A/(nS) for S > 0, with the original zero convention otherwise. The coefficient ωi
already includes the objective weight; it is not multiplied by that weight again. For the root scopes
considered here, the pricing epigraph has γL = 0, so its original inventory objective is J. Denote

Thus FI ≤ FP . The integer-inventory pricing and proof-consumption studies refine FI and its
scope-valid contribution to the complete GI-TBC ledger. Their inventory upper bounds do not
supply vehicle routes.
    This separates the roles of structural relaxation and physical search. Integer inventory states,
normalized ratio products and qualified pricing can strengthen the original objective relaxation
while leaving routing unresolved. A root inventory lower that covers the entire improving domain
can supplement the complete search’s physical lower in the proof ledger. The search may continue
to use its own bounds for adaptive-mass decisions: a stronger proof floor need not change interval
branching, native rows or incumbent production. Conditional child floors instead require complete
child coverage and their corresponding local scopes. These proof-placement choices are separate
from whether a good inventory can be turned into a route.

Proposition 1. Suppose LI ≤ FI ≤ J(Yb ) = UI . If an inventory with objective UI has a verified
complete physical fleet, then LI ≤ FP ≤ UI and 0 ≤ FP − FI ≤ UI − LI .

Proof. Restricting the inventory domain to physically realizable vectors cannot lower its minimum.
The checked fleet supplies a member of that restricted domain with value UI . Combining the two
inequalities gives the result.

     The conclusion is about the recorded domain. If D is an improving domain, its incumbent-cutoff
complement must also be covered before a global certificate is asserted. A pricing tolerance of 10−4
is not substituted for the original complete-method certification tolerance. In particular, infeasibility
of Yb alone does not imply FP > UI : other inventories can have the same or slightly greater price.

Partition stations by identical actual coefficients (ci , ωi ). For a class C, let mCv be the multiplicity
of inventory value v in Yb . The class family is encoded by

                   v                      v             i∈C

using only each original station’s permitted support. The original hard inventory total and all
physical conditions retain their meaning.
Proposition 2. Every member of this family has the same S, A, G, H, J and unweighted inventory
total as Yb .
Proof. Within a class, ci is constant, so preserving inventory multiplicities preserves its ratio multiset.
Taking the union across classes preserves the global ratio multiset, hence S, A, G. Constant ωi also
preserves the class sum of absolute deviations from one. Inventory multiplicities directly preserve
the unweighted total.

    Initial inventories and geographical locations need not coincide within a class. Consequently, the
required pickups, deliveries and routing difficulty can change. This is a practical use of objective
invariance, not a new general symmetry or Benders theorem. Coefficients are compared by their
exact binary representations, without epsilon grouping. The preselected best RX35 family has six
legal members and total pickup 56–57; the best NB35 family is a singleton. This is a small finite
opportunity, not an established large symmetry space.

4    Resource explanations and objective-level coverage
A necessary resource projection can preserve free station/vehicle attribution while replacing exact
tours by lower bounds. Let ℓ(S) be a downward-rounded shortest depot tour through a small station
subset S, computed from exact rational shortest paths of the actual travel coefficients. A route
visiting S has travel at least ℓ(S), even if it also visits other stations. For vehicle service indicators

Together with T ∗ ≥ (tp + td )Pk , a subset row is active only when all its stations are served by that
vehicle. Singles, pairs and triples provide a finite, reproducible family of such conditions. A qualified
resource lower greater than T can rule out its inventory condition; a feasible resource assignment is
not a complete route.
    To establish a physical objective gap, the exclusion must cover a whole level, for example all
original physical solutions with J(Y ) ≤ τ . Infeasibility of a valid necessary relaxation of that entire
level is sufficient; infeasibility of one inventory or one class family is not. A level-specific proof is
consumed only in that level and its qualified coverage. If new exclusions delete an old inventory
upper witness, it cannot remain an upper bound for a newly strengthened inventory problem.
Logical no-goods and infeasibility learning retain their classical attribution [1, 2]; no replacement
assignment master or unlimited learning loop is presumed here.
    For a proposed complete enhancement, both the inventory and any physical completion must be
generated and paid within that method’s own run. An offline route establishes physical possibility,
but its reuse would not measure an independently paid algorithm. At a common outer time origin,
the absolute-gap difference decomposes as

                      (UR − LR ) − (UE+ − LE+ ) = (UR − UE+ ) − (LR − LE+ ).

Thus a reported improvement can be attributed to upper-bound production, lower-bound proof,
or both without equating stronger inventory prices to faster routing. Unachieved certificate times
remain undefined. Stable GI-TBC remains the reference while additional mechanisms are evaluated.


5    Evidence from near-optimal inventory states
The two prespecified roles use their original physical data: RX35 has three vehicles with capacities
(20, 25, 30) and T = 6300 seconds; NB35 has the same capacities and T = 7200 seconds. Their best
saved inventory prices are approximately 0.23612171 and 0.39604655, respectively. The corresponding
inherited physical uppers are 0.29966940 and 0.45029150. These inherited endpoints identify the
unresolved physical opportunity; they are not fresh complete runs.
    At a deterministic capacity of 4096 nodes, the exact fixed-inventory fleet models find no route
and return unknown on both roles. Their measured outer costs are 1507.17 and 1285.90 seconds.
In contrast, the necessary resource model proves a time lower of 6300.62843 seconds for the complete
six-member RX35 family. After the inherited time-proof margin of 0.063 seconds, the lower remains

are therefore impossible under the original time limit. This certificate costs 181.39 outer seconds.
The difference in these costs compares two formulations; it does not isolate a speed benefit from

    For NB35, the resource call at 4096 nodes instead gives a guarded time lower of 6993.96882
seconds, below 7200. Its relaxed assignment upper of 7245.92316 seconds is not a fleet-time upper,
because it has no complete tour. The singleton inventory remains unknown. Neither this unresolved
query nor the RX35 family certificate changes a global original-objective bound by itself.
    The resource obstruction has a concrete physical interpretation. Every member of the RX35
best family preserves total station inventory equal to the initial total. Since vehicles start empty, all
return loads are zero and each vehicle’s pickups equal its deliveries. The maximum individual station
service is seven bikes, below every vehicle capacity, so the simple individual-assignment capacity filter
is inactive. The exclusion already follows from per-vehicle balance, handling and the small-subset
travel inequalities, before exact route order and prefix loads are imposed. Conversely, a separate
metric tour lower combined with minimum aggregate handling gives 17793.65 seconds, below the
fleet budget 18900. Aggregate handling and travel alone do not prove this family impossible.
    To test propagation beyond a family, the same necessary conditions were appended to the
complete original inventory domain, preserving the normalized objective formulation. An unfixed
valid physical-route start and the qualified prior inventory lower were supplied. The revised
diagnostic was stopped after 4099.31 outer seconds with 773 observed nodes: its reported global
bound still remained at the prior floor and its node cost was rising. This was an early evidence-based
reallocation before a proposed 1024-node checkpoint, not exhaustion of the 16384-node capacity.
The earlier cold attempt cost 256.52 seconds and was stopped for the documented information-use
revision. Both incomplete attempts are charged and preserved; neither yields a new objective
certificate.
    The preselected second RX35 family, with 20 legal members and original price approximately
0.23654185, is also excluded by the same necessary model. Its Native time lower 6300.45286 remains
above 6300 after the 0.063 margin; the outer cost is 190.00 seconds. Both complete families,
26 vectors in total, can therefore be removed in a single finite counterfactual pricing batch. A
nonexcluded saved stock of price 0.23685401 supplies a checked upper for that retained inventory
domain, without asserting physical feasibility. This finite exclusion alone cannot raise the old
physical proof lower by more than 0.00080191, about 1.26% of the inherited unresolved physical
gap. This is a ceiling for that particular batch, not a limit on other resource conditions or route
constructions.

    The single finite exclusion batch is repriced with the unchanged original RN objective at 16384
nodes. It costs 200.85 outer seconds and returns the same qualified lower 0.2360521029074557. The
selected complete objective level 0.23648786033514127 remains unresolved. Thus the 26-vector
impossibility proof has not established a strict gap between the global inventory and physical optima.

The two resource proofs plus repricing cost 572.25 seconds before qualification, without a new
physical upper or global lower. Together with the whole-domain diagnostic and the small finite-batch
opportunity ceiling, this provides no sufficiently promising uniform completion mechanism for a
fresh complete algorithm. No complete enhancement is frozen and the conditional eight-arm panel
is not run. Stable GI-TBC remains the default. The family obstruction is a mathematical result;
more general low-price physical realization and objective-level exclusion remain open computational
questions.
