# Inherited uniform M-B contract

Production source content matches all 205 R108 bindings, corresponding to
R107 delivery b5db3f038f64215766a54498d8acc82e384de733. The same qualified
R108 PE is used for every new formal arm; actual PE/DLL rehashes are retained
in baseline/candidate/campaign identities. No production rebuild or source
change occurs. Historical R100 performance identities support inheritance
and never replace a current paired arm.

The three commands are original cold compact P-GRB; complete ENS using
`--algorithm-preset research-round83-vds-equal-net-exchange`; and the same ENS
command plus `--round98-state-service m-binary`. M-B's reported effective
identity is `research-round99-ensc-discrete-structure-m-binary`, not an input
preset. ENS-Q remains false. Alternate representations, LP-G, extra resource,
assignment/STRUCT or route-oracle components remain off. ENS stays the default.

The original full startup, VD-P/F0, physical closure/handoff, AM0.08,
depth/width/cover rules, cutoff epochs, child-cache, milestone and terminal
actions remain. Each arm pays its own startup and owns its physical witness,
scoped lower bounds, Start, route, cut and cache. No instance/category/size,
physical T, wall clock, Work, node or vehicle cost chooses an algorithm mode.

Routes, loads and inventories remain integer; state, assignment and direction
remain binary. M-B relaxes only explicit p/d integer declarations and retains
the exact two state/service linking rows per station stated in
paper_candidate_spec.md. All original numeric rows, bounds, tolerances, full
quantity guards, LP type relaxation/restoration and equal-Q stable Start
normalization remain. The p/d implicit-integrality proof needs integer b/Y,
unique service and binary one-direction service, not extra A/B. LP and
child-LP p/d may be fractional. Missing initial-state selectors mean fixed 0.

The objective/zero-denominator convention, nonnegative inventory/capacity
domains, empty departure, integer nonzero one-way service, prefix/return Q,
loaded return, total-stock/returned-stock relationship, Euclidean/1.5 travel
and full handling/return-unload time are unchanged. min_ratio is compatibility
metadata without a final-inventory lower constraint. Complete certificates
require own physical U and qualified full interval coverage/native returns;
local OPTIMAL, numerical gap or saved leaf label alone never suffices.

The new adapter/runner/evidence changes are outside production: public-table
generation, complete panel/order/Seed bookkeeping, strict per-arm native Seed
readback compatibility, full time/fee receipts and frozen stage judgment.
Evidence-only failures/repairs retain their original bytes and do not change
candidate mathematics, thresholds or performance commands.
