The scientific reading order is [final_report.md](final_report.md),
[mathematical_analysis.md](mathematical_analysis.md), the compact tables in
`reports/`, and the independent records in `review/`. The report and

finished. A design or source-review acceptance is not a completed run.

## New carriers and fixed historical dependencies

`carrier_manifest.json` identifies only the newly produced evidence archives.
Large archives use32MiB transport parts, with both complete-archive and
per-part SHA256 values. Each archive has a member index with exact paths,
lengths and SHA256 values. `public_manifest.json` binds the thin source,
reports, proofs, profiles and archive transport files.

The science carrier commit is fixed before the public readback. Dynamic
closeout records (publication, recovery receipt, cleanup, PR body, staging
list and RESUME) are outside the self-referencing science manifest and may
be added in a later normal metadata commit. Their actual branch head and
PR base/head/Draft are read back separately. Scientific files and carrier
bytes named in the fixed manifest are not replaced by that later closeout.

The role carriers contain current reference matrices, coupled masters,
price models/call/return records, exact-flow proofs, support LPs and selected
primal witnesses. Separate carriers retain qualifications, failures,
source bytes and every outer fee; each complete arm has its own carrier.
Raw originals remain in the active workspace. Executables, DLLs, licenses,
objects and fonts are not packed into these evidence archives.

Old large archives are not repacked. `reuse_index.json` records the required
old member hashes and fixed commits, principally R152 science
d489ec2948f079105e4532a2e06ae4eed9fcd500 and stable R137 science
e735c3447eb1443713c700942d873de1783a4a26. Historical reference does not
authorize a new producer run or charge an old paid experiment again.

## One bounded public readback

After normal push, `scripts/round153_delivery.py restore <fixed-commit>`
uses anonymous HTTPS reads from that exact public commit into a new
`E:/codes/ExactEBRP-work/restore/round153-public-<prefix>-attempt01` directory.
The command is recovery I/O, not an experiment. It checks every downloaded
file and transport part, reassembles each archive, checks every member,
and preserves the attempt directory. It does not call Optimize, a compiler,
Native model loading, an old producer, deletion or moving. The helper's
imports do not launch processes except the explicitly requested curl reads.

The download uses four I/O workers, bounded curl retries and per-request
timeouts. A failed attempt is retained with its failure record; there is
no unbounded retry or fallback performance rerun. The one actual attempt's
fixed commit, file/member counts, hashes and elapsed I/O time are recorded
in `public_restore_receipt.json` or an explicit failure record. Publication
and PR base/head/Draft readback are recorded in `publication.json` and
RESUME when they occur, rather than predicted here.

Local computational reproduction is a different operation: it requires
the explicitly identified executable/runtime and registered launch inputs,
and consumes a new experimental budget. It is not part of the finite
public recovery. The exact measured PE and required DLL are retained
locally under the workspace policy.

## Accounting and bounded closeout

Each experiment/verification child has a launch and closed outer receipt
in `fees/`. Nested H, Native Runtime/Work, candidate-LP and flow timings are
descriptive; they are not added again to that outer total. Compilation and
public I/O are reported separately. Pre-launch snapshot/admission hashing,
budget bookkeeping and receipt formatting outside the outer timer are
disclosed untimed setup/identity checks. No estimated seconds or producer
reruns are used to hide that measurement gap.

After the PR and accepted recovery, `cleanup_record.json` records a bounded
metadata inventory and actual volume-space observations. Zero deletion is
permitted. Unique raw, failed attempts, source bytes, necessary PE/DLL,
the active worktree, user material and old history are retained. The
protected original project is not a cleanup target.
