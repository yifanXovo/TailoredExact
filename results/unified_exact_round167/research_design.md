nonzero visits, Gini products, absolute stock digits and digit products.

## Statements and implementation boundary

Prove the supplied strict conditional counterexample; five legal physical
supports for any digit-conditioned domain; path bijection and logarithmic
size; D167=R_abs in (X,delta,w); arbitrary partial absolute-bit conditioning;
integer physical equivalence; and enhanced containment in C166. Separate
the fixed-g case. The proof uses standard disjunction and network tools:
the new project result is the particular joint stock/service/product/code
object and its conditional information, not invention of these tools.

The standalone driver accepts only the fixed physical entry, representation,
scope, cutoff and prescribed condition level. No AP/count/RN/CDF/theta,
linked-direction, VD-J or other extension options are accepted. Extra or
unsupported arguments fail. R_abs makes the original state selectors
continuous and uses integer absolute delta in tiny MIPs. D167 deletes all
rows touching old state columns and those columns, adds both conserved
flows, and uses no independent legacy product layer. Common rows, column
domains and original objective are exported before/after and byte-hashed.
All new Start columns are mapped from physical inventory, using f=1 and
q=g on its unique path without endpoint division. No opaque Native state
is imported. Actual matrices bind scope, cutoff, representation and masks.

## Frozen matrix and decision rule

The manifest freezes 72 canonical positions (eight inherited R166 frames,
three deterministic masks, three representations), 12 local positions and
four tiny MIPs; at most eight extra Optimize calls are reserved solely for
necessary technical/boundary repairs. All eight frame identities, exact
input/parent SHA, inherited argv, cutoffs and G intervals are taken from the
actual R166 call records. All stations use the selected representation; only
the largest legal domain, lowest-index tie, receives a prescribed F1/F2
mask. Prefix-DAG counting determines both nonemptiness tests without solver
or favorable-objective selection. No mask search is permitted.

Every new Optimize is a C++ call with a flushed begin/end record. Execution
is serial; LP limits are 20s canonical and 5s local, tiny limits 60s; one
thread, Seed0, zero requested gaps, unlimited nodes and otherwise Native
defaults. All integer types are relaxed in raw LPs and restored explicitly.
The aggregate diagnostic outer budget is 3600s, including preparation,
solver-free oracle, failures and checks; the 36000s science window separately
reserves at least 10800s for manuscript and review before diagnostics.

Qualified minimization LPs should give F0 equality and conditional
L_D167=L_R_abs>=L_C166. A local strict counterexample does not guarantee a
strict original-objective increment in the full physical model. Retain
zero increments, infeasibility, failures and unused slots. Size/build and
LP costs are diagnostics, not complete GI-TBC speedups. The next-step
decision uses both actual information increments and representation costs;
O(log C) alone does not justify full integration. No new complete B/P/PS/E+
or Seed panel is authorized. R166's parameter/resource calculations are
used directly, without rerunning them as new science.

One current English paper is reorganized around shared states, the complete
method and certified decisions, targeting 7500-9000 whitespace words. All
necessary positive/adverse evidence remains locatable. The three independent
final reviews are the expressly authorized parallel review work in this goal.
