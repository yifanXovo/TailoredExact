## Problem and proposed change

The fixed Round91 canonical-LP diagnostic found a processing-capacity relaxation gap on C2: its numerical original-F bound rose from 0.60266647 to 0.62264258 with two rounded travel/activation rows. D3 showed no objective gain. This PR prepares one uniform static row family for a later full-method test, without claiming that a root-bound gain makes MIP faster.

For each vehicle, the proposed row bounds total integer pickup by a proved processing allowance times binary depot activation. The first implementation used the emitted canonical duration coefficients. The reviewed repair uses one lower coefficient system shared by raw physical and emitted data, one directed shortest-route computation, and an outward upper horizon covering the unchanged physical acceptance rule. Rounding the quotient upper bound down gives a conservative integer row without a new tuning parameter. The design covers zero handling, no closed route, loaded return, coefficient serialization and unsupported arithmetic.

## Status and validation

The first implementation was **built and structurally qualified; the physical-envelope v2 repair is now implemented and independently source-reviewed but not yet built or tested**. A default-off CLI flag and isolated identity enable the canonical rows, an exact-key cache owned by one algorithm run, and a model-bound row ledger. The original ENS-C startup, AM, split depth and solver tolerances are preserved; A1, native B1, LP-G and alternate archive/scheduling combinations are rejected. The frozen Round90 executable remains unchanged. No Round92 Optimize has run.

The [independent G1 review](results/unified_exact_round92/g1_independent_evidence_review.md) accepts the completed zero-Optimize structural checks: pure arithmetic and CLI 2/2, loaded return, unsafe floating environments, cache identity, historical default-off LP SHA, and independent readback of the actual duration and added rows. One negative-test fixture initially reached an earlier legacy-flow rejection; its narrow test-only repair, failed prefix and costs are retained. Attempt 001 measured 36.017s of nested commands; attempt 002 measured 5.907s of complete invocations. The [cost supplement](results/unified_exact_round92/g1_cost_and_staging_supplement.md) states the full elapsed windows and the unrecoverable initial external-build timing boundary.

The same mathematical review found that validity for the rounded canonical duration row does not imply validity for every original physical route: near-unit coefficient normalization followed by integer flooring can cut off a raw-data-feasible loaded return. The [root decision](results/unified_exact_round92/g1_decision.md) gives the exact dyadic counterexample and blocks G2/G3 until a uniform conservative physical/canonical coefficient and rounding-error contract is proved and qualified. Original problem data and tolerances remain unchanged; this draft is not eligible for promotion.

The [physical-envelope contract](results/unified_exact_round92/handling_activation_physical_envelope_note.md) supplies the mathematical construction and candidate-only lawful-witness and floating-environment checks. The [implementation handoff](results/unified_exact_round92/physical_envelope_implementation_handoff.md), [independent source review](results/unified_exact_round92/physical_envelope_independent_static_review.md) and [root acceptance](results/unified_exact_round92/physical_envelope_static_decision.md) bind the corrected source and focused qualification scope. Pre-execution fixture/domain/entry-guard defects are disclosed. Revised executable qualification waits for the D6 compute release; native performance remains held, and the old structural PASS does not certify v2. This repair does not convert the existing approximate canonical model into an exact rational representation of arbitrary real-valued inputs.

R7/R8/R63 related constraints and negative performance evidence are retained. This is an application of known rounding/activation reasoning, not a new general inequality theorem or a full routing-model convex hull. Round90 measurements keep their own frozen identity; there is no promotion or broad long campaign here.
