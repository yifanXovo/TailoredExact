# Secret and license audit

The final Round 56 compact source, scripts, tests, reference data, documentation, and evidence were scanned for high-confidence private-key, AWS access-key, GitHub-token, and OpenAI-style secret patterns. The scan found zero matches.

Round 56 adds project-authored C++, Python, generated benchmark data, documentation, and compact evidence. It adds no dependency manifest, copied third-party source, binary, model, font, license, notice, or other new license obligation. Gurobi, CMake, the compiler toolchain, and Python remain external tools governed by their existing licenses.
