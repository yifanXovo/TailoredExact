## Scientific account and boundaries

GI-TBC means Gini-interval tailored branch-and-cut (historical S2, configuration
ms2). Complete independently paid H, shared integer-inventory/service states,
VD-P, ordered-supply and pair-support/handling rows, objective estimators and
complete improving-interval coverage form one exact method. AP is off,
pair-only support and tau_AM=.08 are retained. The physical problem keeps empty
departure, loaded return/unloading, unique nonzero one-way service and prefix
vehicle capacity. Mathematical assumptions, implicit quantity integrality,
valid row derivations and coverage/termination remain in the appendices.

The registered 120 positions cover 16 base inputs, 20 physical and 24 objective
configurations. Main Seed0 breadth gives GI-TBC 9/12 certificates versus P 7/12
and PS 7/12; positive-resource pairs give 4/8 versus 0/8 and 0/8. All four n35
resource configurations remain open for every method. Shortage floors and
separated T1 lower/T2 upper endpoints establish resource-value relevance while
preserving each method's own certificate and complete cost.

PS's substantive X-35-B reversal and K-35-U's better P primal/worse P dual
endpoint oppose a universal-dominance claim. At the same n35 physical input
and T=6300, the saved rho2 feasible plan improves both G and P_avg over the
saved rho1 incumbent in an offline comparison; no Start or solution crossed
production runs. Uncertified points
are not an exact optimal weight frontier.

Three AM thresholds yield the same observed coarse Seed0 action patterns on four selected roles;
finite gates, logical splits, no-strict terminals and absent gate exposure
remain distinct. This is not general parameter robustness. Conditional
mechanism costs are mixed: C_R deletion materially improves K-15-S certificate
cost at Seed0; all three K-15-S deletion/full time directions reverse at Seed1; all K-35-U row deletions improve U and weaken L. Root-only has higher L
with the same U on the two hard Seed0 roles. Root strengthening, observed
controller activity and complete-method benefit are separate claims. Initial same-domain LP gains are visible for A/B on two roles; no C_R/C_G objective increment exceeds 1e-7 on the four roles. Equal optimal values do not establish inactive, slack or redundant rows.

Preselected Seed1 observations stay outside the main Seed0 denominator.
UC PS and R-B20-S-T1 GI-TBC show material descriptive clock changes; two Seeds
do not estimate a runtime distribution or justify best-Seed selection. Full
tables retain all repeats. Historical R132/R134/R135 results keep their actual
versions and denominators in the [research index](unified_exact_research_index.md).
