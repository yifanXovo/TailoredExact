# Submission positioning and evidence boundary

Checked against primary publisher/editorial pages on 26 October2026 (Asia/Shanghai). The recommendations below are the present authorship assessment, not an editorial decision or a forecast of acceptance. No submission is made.

The [Elsevier TR-E scope description](https://shop.elsevier.com/subjects/journals/social-sciences-and-humanities/social-sciences/transportation) includes logistics, inventory, material handling and analytical optimization methods. The strongest present TR-E account therefore connects final station stock to actual routes, intermediate capacity and loaded-return handling, and interprets effective within-instance penalty weights through legal fleet operations. Geographic coordinates with synthetic stock/targets/weights must remain labelled as such; the study cannot claim observed customer-service or population-equity improvements.

The [Elsevier EJOR description](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/european-journal-of-operational-research/0377-2217) covers original OR methodology and decision-making practice, including theory/methodology and innovative applications. The strongest present EJOR account is an integrated exact algorithm: shared inventory/service states, valid physical and objective links, scoped complete coverage, and fair certification-cost comparisons against both cold and fully paid-start compact controls. Ordinary McCormick/value-disjunction tools are attributed; state labels alone are not claimed to prove a strict relaxation improvement. Adverse and open results remain part of the result.

The [Operations Research optimization/transportation statements](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/editorial-statement/area-editors-statements) evaluate significant contributions across modeling, theory, algorithms, computation or applications and ask optimization work to excel in at least one dimension with wider relevance. They do not impose a requirement to be fastest on every instance. Our assessment is that the narrow exact tiny-reference TC gain is a useful additional result, while the absent incremental real-root gain and unresolved size/attribution/generalization limits do not by themselves substantially close the higher-positioning gap. A distinctive general result, convincing broader computation, or measured practical impact would be a more consequential next advance than requiring another isolated timing win.

Before submission, the minimal author work is to verify the exact external same-problem model/heuristic bibliographic identity (if applicable), confirm data origins and redistribution permissions, answer the named full-text comparison questions only where stronger claims require them, and apply the chosen journal's then-current author/format requirements. The review manuscript explicitly limits the invention claim to the exact-method increment. Formal advisor/coauthor review can assess this contribution without waiting for TC to become a successful production mechanism.
