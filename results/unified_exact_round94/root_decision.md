Open times are censored elapsed times, not certification times. This panel establishes that both ENS-C and LP-G certify three roles while same-protocol P-GRB remains open at the stated cutoffs; it supplies no exact solve-time ratio to P. U6 has better U and L under ENS-C than LP-G, despite both substantially smaller gaps than P. No preregistered severe-risk or P-certificate/LP-open gate fired. A modest loss is not automatically disqualifying, but these four known development/protection roles and one seed are insufficient to establish robust superiority or generalization.

The F2/B50 benefit is consistent with the earlier R90 evidence. The C20 loss also recurs. The old D6 LP-G 3600-second censoring, fresh 7200-second pair with LP-G 6.22% slower, and C2 seed reversal remain part of the overall judgment; they are not erased by this panel. Actual split evidence is narrower than a casual explanation would suggest: six interior LP-G point proposals across four roles yield only two LP-G-point atomic splits, one each on F2/B50. C20/U6 proposals never become atomic splits. A runtime difference cannot be attributed to a split that did not occur, and observed association is not a causal proof.

Do not promote, combine it with H-ACT or the negative startup neighborhoods, alter AM/depth/width, or start a broad long campaign.
