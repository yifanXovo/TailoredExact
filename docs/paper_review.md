## Question and supported contribution

The problem couples final station inventories, ratio-Gini equity, weighted
target deviation, route-prefix loads and handling time. Vehicles depart empty,
may return loaded while paying unloading time, and provide unique nonzero
one-way station service. The depot is excluded from the objective. The direct
same-problem model/heuristic antecedent is the identified project manuscript
*Equity-aware Bike Repositioning in Station-based Bike-sharing Systems*
(HGA-TGBC). The present contribution is the exact method for that setting.

Shared finite inventory states link service and objective information. The main
text proves implicit service integrality and ordered supply compatibility,
explains pair-support/handling inequalities, and describes fully paid startup
and complete improving-domain coverage. Numerical native qualification,
physical feasibility and independent exact-zero finalization are distinguished.
Established disjunction, convexity and product tools are attributed; the claim
is their problem-specific integration and complete-method evidence.

## Strongest evidence and boundaries

The historical R132 twelve-input/two-Seed study records 17/24 S2 versus 9/24 cold
compact P certificates, with 4/12 for independently paid-start compact PS.
Those outcomes retain their original source policy. R134 final confirmation
on six known roles records 12/14 S2, 9/14 P and 2/4 PS certificates. The panels
and policies differ: the fractions are not pooled or compared as a progression
in one success rate. Open observations have no certification time.

The final study retains repeated large complete-cost gains, including the
actual split/child-target role UC-B20-B. It also retains both substantive
X-35-B paid-start compact reversals and open positive-objective K35 endpoints.
Six certified within-instance weight points show an equity/target trade-off on
K-15-S and a stable operating platform on UC-B20-B. These are two within-input
examples, not a population-level sensitivity result. Coordinate/capacity
sources are distinct from synthetic operating fields; there is no field-demand
or demographic-effect claim. Seeds repeat inputs and are not independent inputs.

R135 closes the AP study: two same-domain incremental root LPs and all 32
registered method positions, forming 16 pairs across seven known inputs.
Both methods certify 10/16. AP has no substantive certified-time win and two
substantive adverse Seed0 differences (about +37.20s and +62.39s), mainly in
native MIP. K20 gaps improve; K35 improves U but weakens final L; D13 Seed1 gap
worsens about 13.83%. AP stays **research-only/default off**. Stronger fixed-root
relaxations do not order finite-search lower bounds or proof times. No new
