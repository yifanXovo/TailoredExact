The net-return census is an inherited diagnostic only. Empty departure implies each car's 0<=sum(p-d)<=Q, whereas cumulative pickup may exceed Q. The lower part is explicitly retained; the upper part depends on integral routes in the inherited formulation. Exactly nine R106 C2 FULL raw events exceed the upper bound; STRUCT has none. No such additional inequality is introduced in any R107 formal or optional revision arm.

GLOBAL and FRONTIER each retain a separate run-local physical evidence session. GLOBAL has one production request, so cross-request reuse is vacuous; FRONTIER may reuse physically compatible FEAS/INF/A/B proofs across actual ENS leaves. Both retain one native Optimize per original MIP request. Model/object lifetime, Optimize count, LP continuity, native B&B tree and basis reuse are reported separately.
