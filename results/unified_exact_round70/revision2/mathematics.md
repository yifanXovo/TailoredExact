# Finite decoded descent and exactness scope

A state assigns each station once among ordered vehicle sequences, with route
separators and no changed original station/vehicle data. There are finitely
many such states. The current full deterministic greedy decoder maps a state
to operations and maximizing fitness minusF. Accept only a gain greater than
1e-12. Strict improvement precludes returning to a state at the same decoded
fitness; each seed's descent terminates on this finite state set. Cache entries
must agree with fresh full decodes on the qualified valid-input scope.

An unsuccessful pass must inspect every candidate generated by the declared
guided neighborhood. The approximate single-route objective is an ordering
proxy, not a rejection bound. This establishes exhaustion only of that
neighborhood under the fixed decode map, not every relocation or an optimum
over routing/operations. Stopping after24 completed descents defines the
finite multi-start heuristic, not a seconds/Work/iteration fallback. If the
whole deadline interrupts search, completion is false. A verified numerical
zero uses the inherited original certificate standard; zero requested MIP
gaps alone do not establish rational exactness.

Every admitted UB is independently checked on the original problem, including
initial/final inventories, one nonzero unidirectional service per station,
vehicle load prefixes, empty departure and allowed loaded return, travel plus
pickup/drop handling, and original Gini/penalty/S=0 conventions. A poor or
rejected heuristic candidate cannot remove an optimal solution. The complete
VD-S model and proof frontier cover all relevant Gini intervals determined
from a legal UB, with valid incumbent pruning and complete parent-child unions.
The candidate modifies only how a feasible UB is acquired. This preserves the
same numerical exact-algorithm contract regardless of heuristic quality.
