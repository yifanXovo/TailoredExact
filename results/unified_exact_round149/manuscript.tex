\section{Returning penalty information to state-product envelopes}
\label{sec:penalty-product-feedback}

Our objective is a Gini epigraph term plus a weighted inventory penalty,
$g+\sum_i\omega_i e_i$, where $e_i\geq |c_iY_i-1|$ and $\omega_i$
already includes the penalty multiplier.  The normal heuristic provides a
verified incumbent $U_0$.  Over the root inventory boxes and the necessary
resource interval $B^-\leq\sum_iY_i\leq B$, define
\[
 p_*=\min\left\{\sum_i\omega_i|c_iY_i-1|:
 \ell_i\leq Y_i\leq u_i,\ B^-\leq\sum_iY_i\leq B\right\}.
\]
Clipping each station to its preferred inventory $1/c_i$ and allocating any
resource shortage or surplus in increasing marginal cost $\omega_i c_i$
solves this continuous resource relaxation in $O(n\log n)$ time.
For production, the allocation only selects actual finite nonnegative
dual multipliers $\mu,\nu$.  Outward arithmetic evaluates
\[
 \underline p\leq \mu B^- -\nu B+
 \sum_i\min_{\ell_i\leq y\leq u_i}
 \{\omega_i|c_iy-1|+(\nu-\mu)y\}\leq p_*.
\]
Each station minimum needs only the endpoints and its possible kink.
An independent exact-arithmetic diagnostic verifies the resource primal
and dual optima and the conservative production direction.

The original penalty, resource and incumbent-cutoff rows already imply
$g\leq U_0-\underline p$.  Thus the scalar bound is not new information
for the old continuous model.  Its propagation into the disaggregated
products is nevertheless incomplete.  With state selectors $s_{iy}$ and
products $t_{iy}$, the old block satisfies
\[
 \sum_y s_{iy}=1,\qquad \sum_y t_{iy}=g,\qquad
 \gamma_Ls_{iy}\leq t_{iy}\leq\gamma_Us_{iy}.
\]
Set $\beta=\min\{\gamma_U^0,\operatorname{up}(U_0-\underline p)\}$ and
add $t_{iy}\leq\beta s_{iy}$ for every existing state when the old upper
bound is larger.  The product-sum equation then also gives
$t_{iy}\geq g-\beta(1-s_{iy})$.  Integer completions under the old cutoff
are preserved, since $t_{iy}=gs_{iy}$ and $g\leq\beta$; this argument also
covers feasible nonminimal epigraph completions.

For strictness at the block level, take the old interval $[0,1]$,
$\beta=.6$, $s=(.5,.5)$, $g=.5$, and $t=(.4,.1)$.  This point satisfies
the old block and the scalar bound but violates $t_1\leq .6s_1$.
This is standard product convexification and bound propagation, not a new
general theorem.  The question is whether the specific BRP resource floor
and the observed VD-P propagation gap have useful complete-search value.

Three saved base-D models, on three physical instances, were reoptimized
after adding only the product upper rows.  No old optimum was recomputed
and no variable was fixed to its saved value.  The numerical optimum
increments are $.001033042371$ for K20, $.001917930741$ for K35 and
$.000598602232$ for RX20.  A K35 scalar-only control differs from the
saved optimum by $-3.23\cdot10^{-7}$, within the existing quality tolerance.
These are floating LP qualifications, not exact rational optimality
certificates.  In particular, K35's PF numerical primal--dual diagnostic
difference is about $.000619046$ and is reported with its residuals;
the diagnostic dual still exceeds the saved old primal.  Saved-point
separation, block strictness, optimized LP gain and complete algorithm
benefit are distinct claims.

The complete candidate uses only its own paid heuristic, computes beta
once, and installs the rows before the first formal solve.  It adds no
source solve, column or extra root reoptimization.  It keeps the logical
proof-root interval, AM geometry and closed-cover policy.  Compatible
children inherit the frozen beta; a child upper bound already below beta
needs no additional row.  Construction, readback, presolve and search
costs are paid by each complete arm.  The accompanying final report gives
the entered same-new-executable comparisons, physical incumbents,
externally supported lower bounds, signed gaps, full costs and adoption
decision; cap-near time differences on open runs are not convergence speed.

The stable reference remains R137 GI-TBC.  R147 standalone D has only its
previously established limited candidate status.  Earlier STOCK and
TC+STOCK results, the corrected AM negative anchors, and R148's serious
RB35 incumbent/signed-gap loss remain relevant historical boundaries.
No Shared-S/STOCK/RN combination, AM rescue, extra seed or dynamic
incumbent rebuilding is part of this intervention.
