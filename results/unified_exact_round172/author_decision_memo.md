# Author decision memo

## Manuscript position

**Complete method.** The strongest method claim is the integration of one stock representation for products, handling and nonzero service with physical inequalities, paid startup and complete bound coverage. The breadth and route-time certificate comparisons include independently paid warm compact search as well as cold compact search. The 50-station study extends direct endpoint evidence across six specified configurations at one Seed and cap. Its CITI compact reversal and twelve OPEN outcomes are part of that result.

**Structure.** The compact-support result characterizes the local projection and the interface that preserves it in the complete model. The absolute-code result explains how the same physical projection can coexist with different information after bit fixing. The theoretical objects, the offline network comparison and the paid compact-support test support different claims. The same-build compact variant loses a certificate and slows X20, and the network prototype uses larger matrices on the tested frames.

**Transportation decisions.** The operator can use two-sided bounds to judge whether extra route time supplies a required reduction in the inventory dispersion and deviation objective. The four matched pairs prove a strict improvement, with wide magnitude intervals at 35 stations. Over a covered weight range, the fleet envelope and lower chords quantify the quality of keeping or switching a plan while respecting empty departure, prefix capacity and unloading. The values describe an objective over archived geometry and synthetic or transformed operating data.

## Journal framing and scientific limits

Use EJOR's method and decision framing for author review. The shared representation, conditional information and method coverage form the method argument. The bounded route-time and weight decisions show what that method contributes to planning. TRE is a meaningful application framing because these are specific inventory and routing decisions with interpretable resource bounds. This is an editorial judgment about scope. It does not characterize when a shared local hull improves complete routing search or when conditioning pays for its extra rows and columns. The closed compact, conditional prototype and fixed reweighting directions retain their negative or mixed evidence.
