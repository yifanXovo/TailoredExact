## 4. J2's strict difference and the exact objective gap

J2's complete four-station integer universe satisfies `d_(0,3)+d_(1,2)<=1`, with vehicle indices 0/1 and station indices 1..4. The full 59 joint fleets attain maximum 1. The saved reference point takes 3/2; the original 24-dimensional direction remains an independent `15/2>7` witness. Its inventory `(1,1,1,0)` is physically attainable, so this is a strict service-coordinate difference, not a strict inventory-projection witness. External donors/receivers cannot be deleted to transplant it to a larger instance.

For J2/J3, integer initial total stock is 3, targets and weights are 1. Complete service hulls preserve nonnegative vehicle returns, so tau=sumY<=3 and sum e>=4-tau>=1, with g>=0. The saved four equal joint-column weights attain Y_i=3/4, e_i=1/4, g=0. Full Fraction checks of the actual loaded binary64 matrices verify all 583 rows, 284 J2 bounds and 298 J3 bounds with no violation. Thus both loaded LP optima are exactly

\[
\widehat\lambda=\frac{5404319552844595}{36028797018963968}.
\]

For nonnegative integer inventories with common target D, a unit transfer between levels differing by at least two reduces the extremal pair distance and never increases third-point distance sums. With `q=floor(tau/n)`, `m=tau-nq`, this gives `G>=m(n-m)/(n tau)` for tau>0. Equal-weight penalty is minimized by the same balanced inventory; the tau=0 branch is treated separately. In this four-station case the tau0/1/2/3 bounds are `4lambda`, `3/4+3lambda`, `1/2+2lambda` and `1/4+lambda`. Legal fleets attain tau0 and balanced tau 3 within both handling budgets. Therefore

\[
F^*_{\mathrm{IP}}=\min\{4\widehat\lambda,1/4+\widehat\lambda\}
=1/4+\widehat\lambda,
\qquad F^*_{\mathrm{IP}}-F^*_{\mathrm{LP}}=1/4.
\]

The coefficient is binary64, displayed 0.15; ideal decimal 3/20 separately gives LP 3/20 and IP 2/5. The saved four mixture fleets are not all objective 0.40. Service convexification does not convexify the integer objective epigraph; the gap already exists on fixed positive tau 3, without claiming a global Jensen property for the variable-denominator objective. This is an explanatory standard balancing fact, not a new general majorization theorem, native sensitivity experiment or production separator.
