# Author decision memo: current exact-method manuscript

The current paper is ready for a substantive author/colleague review. Its
value is a specified integrated exact method for the inherited equitable
redistribution problem, and a precise account of which inventory information
survives the transition to physical routes and paid certification. This is
not a claim that the paper has met a journal's acceptance threshold.

1. **Main method and direct performance claim.** Keep uniform GI-TBC/B as
   the main method. The breadth panel gives B9/12 versus P7/12 and PS7/12;
   the separately designed resource pairs give B4/8 versus 0/8 and 0/8.
   On the later six-input cohort, each of three solver Seeds gives B3/6
   versus P1/6; all n35 observations remain OPEN. These are finite results
   for the complete package and specified controls. E+ is a separately
   paid proof extension: six Seed0 physical U equal B, only X35 final L
   improves, and no certificate is added. Only C20's roughly 26.10 s paired
   increase is substantive under the registered scale. A proof-priority
   user may reasonably prefer uniform E+; the selected main-paper tradeoff
   favours B. No per-input B/E+ portfolio or new E+ Seed claim is justified.

2. **Theoretical and mechanistic contribution.** The shared inventory
   selector connects products, absolute movement and nonzero visits.
   Unique assignment, binary direction, integer stocks and balance imply
   integer service without A/B. Ordered supply, integer handling and the
   true-Gini physical embedding give problem-specific valid information.
   Cutoff complements and complete coverage explain correctness. Product
   expansion, value disjunction, McCormick/Jensen and partitioning remain
   established tools. An isolated VD-P product has the rectangle projection;
   the linked model is not a route hull. Small conditional A/B root gains,
   mixed deletion costs and Seed reversals explain the limits of current
   mechanism evidence. The outer scheduler has no separately established
   substantive full-over-root certificate-time benefit in the tested roles.

3. **Most important substantive gap for an OR-level claim.** The current
   evidence does not show that outer interval control contributes an
   independent computational advantage over the same strengthened root
   formulation with the same own startup. Nor do the valid stock/service
   links yet provide a broad characterization of when that connection
   changes the physical objective gap or net complete cost. These are
   specific missing explanatory results, not a request for a larger Seed
   table. The paper is assessable now because it states those objects,
   supplies complete models/proofs and retains adverse controls. C35's
   historical P fleet is better than every B incumbent despite its reversed
   within-Seed ordering; the new X35 observations still miss the frozen
   absolute target. Additional pricing precision alone cannot explain
   these physical primal deficits.

4. **Next scientific decision.** Review this manuscript first; start no
   new experiment automatically. If the authors want a further project,
   the single informative candidate is an architecture decision between
   full GI-TBC and the already defined strengthened root-only continuation.
   It must use one uniform, prospectively fixed comparison contract, retain
   own H, inclusive physical coverage, paid lifecycle, both endpoints and
   null OPEN times, and judge substantive certificate/cost differences
   alongside open bounds. Evidence of a reproducible incremental full-method
   benefit would support retaining outer control as a performance mechanism;
   comparable certificates/endpoints at lower complete cost would support
   simplification. It must not select favourable instances or thresholds to
   manufacture an outer win. Existing full/root and R62/R96/R97/R160 work
   rule out presenting a renamed route/quantity oracle as a new question.
