\section{Active Sparse Fixed-Interval Formulation}
The active families below are generated by the canonical
\texttt{paper-k1-am-sf} preset. They are static rows, variable domains, or an
extended formulation; none is a dynamic user-cut callback.

\subsection{Interval and product structure}
For $G=H/(nS)$ and $S>0$, every fixed interval satisfies
\begin{equation}
 H\leq n\gamma^U S,\qquad H\geq n\gamma^L S. \label{eq:direct-gini}
\end{equation}
If $a\in\{0,1\}$, $G\in[\gamma^L,\gamma^U]$, and $w=Ga$, the four rows
\begin{align}
w&\geq\gamma^L a,&w&\leq\gamma^U a,\\
w&\geq G-\gamma^U(1-a),&w&\leq G-\gamma^L(1-a)
\end{align}
give the exact binary product hull. K1--AM--SF applies this hull to the binary
expansion of final inventory, together with tight denominator bounds.

Let valid domains be $P\in[P^L,P^U]$ and $S\in[S^L,S^U]$. The auxiliary
$W_{SP}$ uses the four McCormick rows for $SP$ and the paper-safe estimator
\begin{equation}
 H+n\lambda W_{SP}\leq n(\cutoff-\eps)S. \label{eq:sp-estimator}
\end{equation}
The simpler objective estimator and the penalty lower-bound closure are also
active. Final-inventory penalty domains, movement-reachability domains, and two
deterministic domain-propagation rounds tighten bounds without removing any
original feasible point.

\subsection{Inventory, visits, duration, and connectivity}
Inventory conservation includes
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i b_i-\sum_k Q_k\leq\sum_iY_i\leq\sum_i b_i.
\end{equation}
The visit links
\begin{align}
Y_i-b_i&\leq(c_i-b_i)\sum_kz_{ki},\\
b_i-Y_i&\leq b_i\sum_kz_{ki}
\end{align}
force $Y_i=b_i$ when no route visits station $i$. Exact small-support travel
lower bounds generate pair and triple support-duration covers. A
vehicle-indexed connectivity-flow extended formulation links selected arcs and
visits. The verified-incumbent objective row is an upper-bound cutoff only; it
does not import lower-bound evidence.

The exhaustive station-subset duration block is deliberately absent. Gini
spread, required-movement, transfer-cutset, subset-inventory, dynamic
support-duration, and all other tailored callback families are inactive in the
stable preset. Earlier sections or experiments using those families are
historical and are not evidence about K1--AM--SF.

\subsection{Audited inventory--route research family}
Round 54 separately considered, for nonempty station $A$ excluding the depot,
\begin{align}
\sum_{i\in A}(Y_i-b_i)&\leq\sum_k Q_k\delta_k^-(A),\label{eq:ir-in}\\
\sum_{i\in A}(b_i-Y_i)&\leq\sum_k Q_k\delta_k^+(A).\label{eq:ir-out}
\end{align}
Summing vehicle load conservation inside $A$ shows that net delivery is at
most the load entering $A$, and entering load is at most route capacity; the
reverse argument proves \eqref{eq:ir-out}. Valid interval bounds
$L_i\leq Y_i\leq U_i$ give projected companions with right-hand sides
$\max\{0,\sum_{i\in A}(L_i-b_i)\}$ and
$\max\{0,\sum_{i\in A}(b_i-U_i)\}$. Exact directed min-cuts separated these
rows externally at the root. They strengthened many root relaxations but
failed the frozen live gate, so they remain inactive research families.
