2    A bounded simultaneous inner domain
For vehicle k, let Pk and Dk be cumulative pickups and deliveries. Empty departure gives return
Rk = Pk − Dk , with 0 ≤ Rk ≤ Qk . Including return unloading,

Cumulative Pk may exceed Qk . Only each single station operation and the return are bounded by
vehicle capacity in the resource model; physical prefix loads are checked separately.
    The short fixed-owner reference optimizes original J over full supported quantities on the initial
disjoint owner sets Ak . It allows zero service and direction changes, while initially unassigned
stations retain Yi = bi . A full-Ak three-stop budget remains conservative after zeroing a station. This
is an inner restriction, not exhaustive fixed-owner quantity search. The original RN centering/scale
and one-sided envelope are retained.
    The joint mechanism releases all supported quantities and eligible owners on eight stations
at once. Every outside quantity and nonzero owner remains fixed, with its full pickup, delivery
and return contribution included. Conditional active-subset budgets are rebuilt for every vehicle.
Activation, zero service, direction changes, cross-car ownership and unequal-net quantity/ownership
compensation are permitted without inheriting an uncovered old active-set budget.

    1. Score station i by its best unconstrained single-stock reduction in true J, multiplied by one
       plus current owner handling pressure.

    2. Choose the step-ranked anchor, then seven stations by that score plus anchor-weighted
       geometric proximity; break ties by station index.

    3. Solve the joint original-J subproblem with a complete feasible Start, Seed 0, Threads 1 and at
       most 512 nodes. Retain strict true-J improvements and read them back independently in the
       complete original resource domain.

    4. Stop after 12 blocks or the ordinary whole-method deadline. No nondecreasing intermediate
       move or time-triggered algorithm switch is used.

    Both roles use the same rule and capacities. The short references use 2048 nodes each. The
declared domain can coordinate eight changes beyond one single-stock change, owner move/swap or
pair transfer. This is not a reachability theorem against every sequence of historical moves. Trace
evidence distinguishes allowed freedoms from those actually exercised. Every subproblem lies inside
DR . Its feasible point can give a global resource upper; its bound, optimum or failure remains local.
None enters the global proof ledger.
    The new repeated model construction, block selection, callback and completion are C++. The
standalone tool loads an explicitly exported, unchanged qualified RN objective;
