## Problem and proposed study

ENS-C's physical closure already searches single-station changes and equal-transfer two-station quantities. It does not exhaust the same-direction line that increases or decreases both station inventories together. With nonsmooth fractional Gini, a point can resist both existing directions while improving along this third direction. This round studies that one family, preserving original route feasibility and objective evaluation, all 24+1 starts and all proof-controller settings.

The [bounded continuation](research_plans/continuation_three_rounds_2026-09-28.md) permits at most three rounds. [Round93's plan](results/unified_exact_round93/plan.md) selects full integer co-direction enumeration before any experiment; arbitrary independent two-coordinate blocks and unproved analytic skipping are not part of this implementation. No default changes, instance switches, internal resource slices, historical-start injection or LP-G/H-ACT combination are proposed.
