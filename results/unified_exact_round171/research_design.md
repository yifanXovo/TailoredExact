# A prospective n50 complete-method comparison

Round170 qualified 22 positions with 41 paid H calls and 84 Optimize calls
(57 LP and 27 MIP). The four certificates were all on C20. All 18 n35 arms
remained open. At the registered descriptive scale, fixed reweighting
improved final U in two of the six n35 C/B pairs and absolute gap in one.
No n35 role retained a lower final U at both new Seeds. The C20 certificate
process-time ratios were 1.00657 and 1.33224. Same-weight restarts kept
the initial and final B upper in all three Seed1 n35 roles. The three
fresh B/P comparisons at Seed1 improved U by 54.60%, 34.33% and 19.90%,
respectively, and improved qualified L and both gaps. Those are three
comparison observations on exposed inputs. The fixed candidate line is
closed. B remains the uniform main method. C166, D167 and C169 remain off.

The prospective question is how the **complete** frozen pair-only B
compares with truly cold compact P-GRB on six new positive-target n50
configurations. CITI, CABI and Divvy each supply an archived compact and
regional station set. The two sets within each source are row-disjoint.
Depot transformation, initial inventory, target and weights are synthetic.
Each input uses three vehicles with capacities 20, 25 and 30, rho=1 and its
own serialized-weight lambda and T1 horizon. The process cap is 1,200 s
per arm at Seed 0, with serial block order and one paid B initialization.
All six are included regardless of outcome. Geometry, operational
quantities and T vary across the design, so these are finite configuration
observations. The study leaves scaling laws, Native Seed stability and
the outer package's independent performance effect unresolved.
