## Mathematical and actual-model result

Distinct-station threshold events reuse original VD-P inventory selectors. A true event belongs to its actual unique service vehicle. Empty departure and nonnegative loaded return imply total pickup at least total delivery; subset time therefore needs a safe travel lower bound plus `c*max(pickup thresholds,delivery thresholds)`. Q limits a single operation, never cumulative pickup. Extra suppliers, receivers, visits, arbitrary legal orders and loaded return remain possible.

Complete small-support necessary-assignment DP gives the exact rank of a relaxation at support<=9 (standalone<=10). Scalable directional resource counts plus complete eligibility-slot matching give an upper rank for large supports. Every genuine assignment embeds in these systems, so the sum of event selectors is at most that rank. Candidate search is finite and heuristic; proofs are complete for their stated systems. No route sufficiency or exact separation of the whole family is claimed. The generic cover, DP, Hall/matching and API principles are established methods. This work concerns their resource/matrix contract, existing-selector mapping and sparse execution on this BRP stack. See [algorithm](mathematical_algorithm.md), [history/delta](method_delta.md) and [primary related work](related_work.md).

The actual imported matrix is audited once per MIP: binary one-hot state and inventory links, unique service/direction, quantities, route connectivity/MTZ, nonnegative return and complete duration rows. Common lower coefficients are no greater than either physical or exported coefficients. Outward arithmetic and a safe horizon protect exclusions; raw original-column activity separately proves reliable violation. Node-local bounds/fixing, incumbent cutoff and G values are not cut-validity premises. No new variables, static rows, quantity types or objective terms are introduced.

Four complete actual ENS LPs contain selected rows not implied by their full imported LPs. The F5 example has38 pickup events at q12 and rank16, while all703 pairs admit physical routes on every car. This is beyond the all-pairs-incompatible clique structure, without a universal projection/native-cut dominance claim. Adding two selected rows gives no original-objective LP improvement on any of the four roles, and all four fixed common-coordinate points reextend. Selector-vector nonredundancy must not be promoted into objective or common-projection improvement. The LP bank used16 Optimize calls; reextension used4. See [qualification](qualification.md).
