# Finite current-round reproduction

## Entry and scope

[RESUME.md](RESUME.md) and RESUME.json record the fixed science commit and the actual public recovery receipt after publication. The standalone transport entry is scripts/round135_public_restore.py. It accepts a fixed 40-hex commit, a new owned destination under E:/codes/ExactEBRP-work/restore, and a receipt path outside that destination. A failed transport resumes in the same owned failed root with the same arguments and --resume.

The recovery fetches only the explicitly listed current files/carriers and a few fixed dependencies. It replays all 32 entered R135 positions and two AP roots, with no historical whole-campaign recovery. The original measured production, verification and certification clocks are preserved; replay duration is a separate delivery cost.
