## Evidence and scope

The complete independently bounded two-station inventory neighborhood contains the old single-coordinate, equal-transfer and Round93 co-direction quantity moves for a fixed served pair. It holds owners and visit order fixed, removes zero operations, and evaluates original physical feasibility and F. The microexample demonstrates a strict gain unavailable to those narrower directions.
