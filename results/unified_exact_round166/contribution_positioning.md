# Scientific contribution and scope

The paper's main claim is a complete exact method for the stated equitable
bicycle-redistribution operations, organized around a common inventory
interface. It joins a feasible physical allocation, a product/objective
representation, movement/visits, and complete true-Gini regional coverage.
The decision analysis turns retained feasible fleets and qualified global
bounds into statements that remain meaningful between sampled weights and
when individual time-budget problems are open. Audit volume is not a
scientific contribution.

## Inherited model and actual increment

The physical problem, inventory-to-target ratio dispersion, weighted target
deviation, and HGA-TGBC decoder lineage remain attributed to the antecedent
author work. The available complete antecedent is the May30 draft, whose
identity is checked in [antecedent_check.json](antecedent_check.json). It is
not represented as the latest published article and is not redistributed.
GI-TBC adds shared-state formulation connections, physical valid rows,
objective-preserving embeddings, a complete exact controller and paid
comparisons against compact controls. It does not claim to invent the BRP,
the decoder, Gini fairness, disjunctive programming or branch-and-cut.

The implicit service-integrality result uses unique nonzero one-way service
and integer final inventory. The shared movement/visit equations strengthen
the continuous formulation but are not premises of that lemma. The full
method's coverage proof concerns every improving physical point and includes
the objective-cutoff complement; a local native bound alone is insufficient.
Its implementation remains numerically qualified rather than an exact
rational certificate engine.

## The representation question answered in Round166

For contiguous legal integer stocks, the joint graph visible through stock,
absolute movement, nonzero visits and the G-times-stock product has at most
five legal support stocks. The proof gives both interpolation directions,
a constructive LP bit lift, an exact integer extension, and an interface
condition for embedding into the complete model. Capacity-independent support
applies only to the continuous breakpoint block: the independent absolute
binary-product layer still costs O(log C).

This is a specific, transferable structural result built with standard
piecewise-affine disjunction and binary-product tools. The five-generator
necessity statement concerns selection of legal graph-point generators, not
arbitrary extended-formulation complexity. The boundary is substantive:
holed domains need separate legal-domain enforcement, and additional
state-dependent functions need their common affine pieces. Continuous
breakpoints plus integer stock fail the exact product; binary breakpoint
weights remove legal interior stocks. These counterexamples preclude two
tempting but invalid simplifications.

The actual C++ C166 implementation replaces the continuous state block and
retains the original absolute bit/product layer, e epigraph and all physical
rows. Default B is unchanged. Old LOG reduced selector bits while retaining
all continuous states; resource-row aggregation and physical product
completion answer other questions. The targeted history checks are in the
[design](research_design.md) and independent mathematics reports. Equality
of the raw common LP projection precedes solver-generated cuts; it does not
predict presolve, branching, certificate counts or full costs. The sole
six-input same-build Seed0 comparison and its adoption decision are in the
[complete result summary](reports/summary.json) and [final report](final_report.md).

## New decision knowledge

Fifty complete historical fleet records are physically rechecked; 32 belong
to the four fixed weight families and 24 to four time pairs, with six shared
records. All ownership/source records survive deduplication. No new solver
call produces these decision results.

The exact finite-intersection calculation establishes continuous-range
absolute regret bounds for the archive envelope. K15 has three supported
component lines and bound .002356282; UC-B20 has one line throughout and
bound 1.0000101e-7; B20-T1 has three supported lines and bound .015594269;
X35-T2 has one supported line but a remaining bound .136115162. These are
outward display bounds after explicit source-error margins. They describe
best retained feasible plans, not a solved Pareto frontier or the online
performance of any method at an unsampled weight.

All four time pairs have positive certified objective-improvement lower
bounds, approximately .282262, .210529, .111261 and .060086. The first two
two-sided intervals are narrow; the n35 widths remain about .261271 and
.321218. The operations show that improved objectives can coexist with
increased loaded return (B35:2 to3), whereas X35 changes1 to0. These are
route descriptions and objective-value bounds, not monetary benefits,
causal optimality mechanisms or general marginal resource prices.

Re-evaluating the existing high-weight X35 fleet at the original unit-weight
coordinate improves the known feasible value by .06676618239. It diagnoses
an old incumbent deficit; it changes neither historical U nor discovery/

## Evidence retained and claims withheld

Historical breadth/resource denominators, the three-Seed six-input cohort,
C35's primal reversal, all n35 open outcomes, X35's unmet fixed target,
E+'s proof gain and cost, and the unresolved independent speed value of
the outer controller remain visible. New C166 runs are separately named;
old B/P results are not relabelled as measurements of the new executable.
Six fresh B controls yield no new strict B/P comparison and no independent
new physical input or Seed value.

The work now supports an EJOR-style exact-method and decision-evidence
paper, with a useful TRE emphasis on transport/inventory/handling and
bounded resource decisions. The structural compression is not by itself
evidence of an exceptional general optimization advance. Remaining
scientific limits are specific: native search consequences of equivalent
representations, quality gaps on the n35 parameter family, and the
incremental computational value of outer control. No further research round
is launched by this delivery.
