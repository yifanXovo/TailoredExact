# Targeted original reading and applicability

Internal research record,15 November2026. Public manuscript prose describes
the literature's actual assumptions; it does not reproduce reading logs.
The reading is bounded to the two specified originals and direct claims.

1. Erdogan, Laporte and Wolfler Calvo(2014), EJOR238(2),451–457,
DOI10.1016/j.ejor.2014.04.013. Read the Bath hosted preprint, title date
3 April2014,21 PDF pages including repository cover:
https://purehost.bath.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/87724146/bike_repositioning_post_refereeing.pdf.
Read Introduction's precise definition and section2.1/2.2, author pages3–5
(PDF5–7), equations(1)–(9) and the transition to section3. Their one-vehicle
demand-interval problem admits a fixed-route min-cost network even with
handling costs. Depot inventory and travel/handling objective conventions
are specified. This is a clear antecedent, not a theorem that the present
multi-vehicle Gini-plus-penalty objective separates by vehicle. In the
current model the ratio pairs and their common denominator remain global;
optional zero service also changes compressed travel under the resource T.

2. Szeto and Shui(2018), TRB109,176–211,
DOI10.1016/j.trb.2018.01.007. Read the HKU hosted open-access36-page
article at https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/259227/1/content.pdf:
section2.1 problem definition (journal179), section2.2 model/constraint
explanation, sections3.1–3.3 (journal183–186), and section3.4.1's new
loading rule and explanation (journal186–187). Their stated priorities are
excess total-demand dissatisfaction then service time; stations are assigned
once and the depot keeps no bikes. Section3.1 explicitly requires empty
departure and return, hence equal total pickup/drop on each route.
Section3.2 transforms the priority objective and section3.3 uses surplus/
deficiency-bounded quantities. Section3.4.1 explains why minimum-dissatisfaction
greedy loading can carry unnecessary stock under the service-time priority.
These conditions differ from current arbitrary legal final stocks, global
Gini/weighted deviation and paid loaded returns. No greedy-optimality result
is transferred. This memo does not claim to audit all later propositions
or every experimental result.

No fixed-route optimization/network/greedy tool is claimed as original to
Round168. Current mathematical claims concern exact full-family scope,
objective-safe decoding and directionally qualified numerical evidence.
