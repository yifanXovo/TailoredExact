The canonical P writer, parser and evaluator define the current original physical model. Names such as CplexBaseline.cpp, HGA-TGBC or F0 are historical implementation labels, not proof of an external paper's precise ancestry. Relevant code/comment inspection did not supply a complete public-paper-to-P mapping; the draft therefore treats the current frozen source as its model authority. The nine external papers provide positioning rather than substituted model provenance.

The H bridge calls the inherited full decoder and the project later added joint insertion, strict descent, physical closure, balanced relocation and equal-net exchange. The evidence being reused concerns that complete paid H, not a claim of newly authored general genetic search. Historical paid-start/backend controls are cited only for their actual scoped attribution. Unexposed rows and unchanged startup results cannot identify an effect of a newly enabled backend feature.

Gurobi is responsible for native optimization, presolve, cuts and branching. The project is responsible for the problem-specific representation, valid row assembly, own feasible startup and controller/evidence integration. Local coefficient/physical checks are meaningful supports, but cryptographic identity by itself is not a mathematical contribution.
