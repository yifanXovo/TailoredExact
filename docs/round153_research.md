# Round153: complete inventory objective hull and own-root floor

This study continues R152 on the same complete free routing relaxations.
The [adopted goal](../results/unified_exact_round153/goal.md) requires both
meaningful whole-hull value bounds and a separately paid test of a uniform
scalar-floor opportunity. Scientific evidence and publication status are
recorded separately in the [decision](../results/unified_exact_round153/candidate_decision.json)
and [RESUME](../results/unified_exact_round153/RESUME.md).

The final coupled LP intervals are [0.39604305,0.39604656] on N-B35-S,
[0.28285969,0.28732617] on K-35-U and [0.18332184,0.18995681] on N-C20-U
(display endpoints rounded outward). NB35 meets its accuracy target.
K35 and NC20 retain quantified uncertainty after targeted long prices,
changed directions, same-Q support transfer and large true-column probes.
They establish positive value, without a claim of full convergence or an
ineffective family. The [final report](../results/unified_exact_round153/final_report.md)
and machine tables retain all endpoints, unsuccessful alternatives and costs.

The [mathematical account](../results/unified_exact_round153/mathematical_analysis.md)
defines the precise historical reference, the common-stock Gini completion,
restricted-master uppers, full-domain price correction and safe support
rows. It distinguishes the strengthened LP value from physical routing
incumbents. Both old normalized-count inequalities and all four reciprocal
stock-product inequalities are restored, along with the qualified earlier
row union. The independently checked fixed-marginal gap is 41/1080; it is
not a complete-routing strict example.

The [paper insertion](../results/unified_exact_round153/manuscript.tex)
places these results after the stable method and earlier relaxation studies.
The [deepening rationale](../results/unified_exact_round153/deepening_plan.md)
records why dual stabilization, longer prices and exhaustive denominator
partitions were attempted, including unsuccessful comparisons. A wide
interval remains unresolved rather than becoming a negative result.

The uniform [own-root candidate plan](../results/unified_exact_round153/production_plan.md)
uses its own H and actual canonical domain. All oracle and helper costs are
paid by each arm. Its sole formal addition is a qualified total-objective
floor; the research switch defaults OFF. The eight-position matrix has an
[independent admission](../results/unified_exact_round153/review/confirmation_admission.md),
which preceded the experiment. All eight own-H arms completed. NB35 and
RB35 improved L but worsened U/gap; K35 improved U and absolute gap while
weakening L; NC20 returned the same certificate with a small time cost.
The candidate remains research-only and default OFF. Diagnostic LP value
and complete-search benefit are separate findings.

Stable GI-TBC remains the method in the [release guide](paper_s2_release.md).
Its original entry, profile and finite evidence retain their identities.
Current-only evidence carriers and their actual public recovery are linked
by the continuation record; old large archives are referenced at fixed
commits and member hashes rather than repacked or rerun.
