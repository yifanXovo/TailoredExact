# Inherited physical and numerical obligations

The authoritative R112 mathematical note is retained in inherited_r112. Every
new arm independently owns its H, fleet, input inventory, clock and bounds.
Vehicles depart empty; each served station has one nonzero-direction integer
operation and is visited by at most one vehicle. Every prefix and return load
lies in [0,Q_k]. Loaded return and its depot unloading are allowed and paid.
Q is instantaneous capacity, not cumulative pickup; inventory is in [0,C],
without a min_ratio lower inventory constraint. Travel plus (pickup+drop) times
cumulative pickups gives the complete paid route duration.

With positive lambda and weights, the inherited nonnegative F=G+lambda P is zero
iff exact integer inventory Y=D. The original zero-denominator evaluator and
1e-7 numerical closure are preserved, and exact Y=D is reported separately.
G50-C1 already has a legal zero optimum and exact floor0. G100-R2 zero remains
unknown unless a new own legal zero fleet establishes it. An unstarted positive
P-S call supplies no native L or certification: only its own physical H U and
the independent full-domain floor0 survive. A legally submitted complete Start
does not change that fact or alter the original integer compact model.

Actual started P-S still requires the full original model, one returned MIP,
all Start columns/rows/API readback, parameter/domain/lifecycle checks and its
own physical witnesses. ENS/M-B retain original true-G/cutoff/epoch/cover and
integer semantics, including M-B frozen A/B and implicit p/d integrality.
Signed micro-negative gaps and damaged/null claims retain the old policy;
native numerical certification is not a rational optimality proof.

R112_COMPLETE12_PLUS_R113_GEO8 is a stratified five-role finite contribution map.
Old twelve clocks remain their original receipts and old comparisons remain
within R112; the new two four-method groups compare only within R113. Replay
does not change a historical cost. This is neither twenty new-wrapper measures,
R112 completion, independent new geography, nor statistical significance.
