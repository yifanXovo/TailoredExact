\begin{abstract}
We present K1--AM--SF, the stable paper-facing exact method selected by
\texttt{paper-k1-am-sf}. The tailored Gini-interval branch-and-cut framework
starts from one complete improving interval, uses midpoint adaptive-mass
refinement with threshold $0.08$, and solves each unresolved leaf with the
F0--CLEAN sparse tailored fixed-interval MILP under Gurobi's native
branch-and-cut. Dynamic user-cut callbacks and custom branching are off. Exact
interval coverage, scope-valid bounds, and independent incumbent verification
govern certification. A bounded inventory--route cut study strengthened root
relaxations but failed the fixed-interval promotion gate and remains outside
the stable algorithm.
\end{abstract}
