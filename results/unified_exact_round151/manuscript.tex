\subsection{Resource penalty propagation without GD}

GI-TBC is the stable complete method. For actual objective weights, define $h_i(y)=\omega_i|c_i y-1|$. One fixed resource dual yields $d=\mu B^- -\nu B+\sum_i m_i$ and $d_{iy}=\mu B^- -\nu B+\sum_{j\ne i}m_j+h_i(y)+(\nu-\mu)y$. Weak duality gives conditional penalty lower bounds without any GD or STOCK assumption. Outward evaluation and the arm's own cutoff produce upper caps $t_{iy}\le\beta_{iy}s_{iy}$ directly in the existing state products. One row per state covers the uniform information as well; no duplicate PF layer or new column is needed.

The resource relaxation retains the root boxes and $B^-\le\sum_iY_i\le B$. Here $m_i=\min_{\ell_i\le z\le u_i}\{h_i(z)+(\nu-\mu)z\}$, with fixed $\mu,\nu\ge0$. Define conservative floors $p_0=\max\{0,\operatorname{down}(d)\}$ and $p_{iy}=\max\{p_0,0,\operatorname{down}(d_{iy})\}$. Uniform and conditional caps are $\beta_0=\min\{\gamma_U^0,\operatorname{up}(U_0-p_0)\}$ and $\beta_{iy}=\min\{\beta_0,\operatorname{up}(U_0-p_{iy})\}$. For an integer selected state, $Y_i=y$ and $t_{iy}=g$; $g+P_e\le U_0$ and $P_e\ge p_{iy}$ establish the cap even for a nonminimal epigraph completion. Prefix/suffix lower sums avoid subtracting independently rounded bounds. The fixed-multiplier propagation pass is linear in stations plus supported states; the inherited multiplier selection also sorts station slopes.

The same monotone upward coefficient transport is used for uniform and conditional caps, preserving the actual relaxation inclusion $Q_C\subseteq Q_P\subseteq Q_B$. A zero global penalty floor can coexist with nonzero conditional information. Caps are frozen at the first own root and inherited only by compatible subdomains; they do not change the physical objective, root proof interval or AM scheduling rules.

On the same saved pure-B N-B35 root, fully free LP values are $L_B=0.327833772177$, $L_P=0.332161666001$ and $L_C=0.337310427549$. These numerically qualified LP increments motivate a finite complete test rather than establish its outcome. The N-C20 saved optimum already satisfies the new rows and is not reoptimized.
