## What the targeted trials establish

The registered eight-step target loops complete for both K35 and NC20.
Each saved step reports a legal true-stock rejection of its own frozen target;
none proves that target. The successful K35 v2 loop adds eight columns after
the six retained from the original long trial; the earlier K35 v1 selection
failure is not a pricing result. NC20 adds ten columns in its eight-step loop,
then eight more from the matched midpoint screens. Prices from these changing
directions are not one convergence sequence.

For the original saved K35 direction, the strengthened 3600s trial has fresh
price lower `0.06825209584795439`, below the already inherited same-direction
`0.07003842216867079`. Its true price upper `0.07966801611689346` is below
target `0.08077041468890388`; the resulting individual-certificate ceiling is
`0.2861237647323068`. The registered conditional RN-long skip is justified by
that true counterexample. This is not a matched-long speed comparison.

The later NC20 midpoint task provides a more informative structural contrast
than extending a refuted near-upper target. Both oracles use the saved same
direction and price target `0.052369508028890836`, hull target
`0.18659477577615025`, and common initial information:

RN has the stronger returned fresh lower in this pair; the residual finds the

certificate's ceiling to `0.18504946084304422`, below the frozen midpoint
target. The saved `long_skip.json` records
`interior_target_disproved_by_verified_true_stock` and explicitly disclaims a
family/full-hull rejection. Thus omitting the two now-redundant long target
proofs follows the predeclared stopping rule. It neither proves a universal
oracle speed advantage nor closes the full inventory hull. Grouping the same
direction into its one strongest support is appropriate: the RN lower is used
for that support, while the real stocks from both screens are retained.

## Bounded research interpretation

These finite trials support the distinction between a usable target-directed
integer oracle and a material improvement of the accumulated full-hull lower.
They yield legal new columns, an NC20 numerical upper reduction, qualified
finite structural comparisons, and preserved negative/undecided outcomes.
They do not deliver a material new main lower, close K35 or NC20 to `1e-4`,
establish residual dominance over RN, or reject the entire inventory family.

Closing this finite pricing campaign after its registered loops, one original
long trial, and the matched interior screens is consistent with the observed
target rejections. Another long solve against a target already refuted by a
legal stock would not answer the remaining full-hull question. Any later
inventory investigation should require a new observed bottleneck and a newly
frozen proof/value threshold; this assessment authorizes no wider search.

The independent own-H complete-method E experiment is a separate mandatory
comparison. No placement or physical-performance conclusion is drawn while
its nine full arms remain incomplete. Final approval is held until the complete
panel, actual finite audit, fee closeout and final manuscript claim review.
