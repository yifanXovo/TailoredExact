# Contribution scope

Commodity-flow formulations are established routing models. Rieck and Zimmermann
present vehicle-flow and commodity-flow formulations for simultaneous pickup and
delivery ([publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03342743)).
The single-commodity pickup/delivery formulation of Martinovic et al. also uses
continuous arc load ([publisher article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2008/697981)).
These related problems have different delivery/depot/service assumptions, so
their models are not substituted verbatim for BRP.

This stage claims no new commodity-flow theorem or inequality. Its incremental
content is the exact replacement in the current BRP model, the explicit proof
that old load recurrences are implied, preservation of loaded return and
recycled capacity, and matched end-to-end evidence. Mathematical validity,
implementation contribution and measured performance remain separate.
