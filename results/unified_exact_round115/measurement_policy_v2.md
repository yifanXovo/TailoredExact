Frozen before finite qualification and before all four new G100 arms. This explicitly changes the research measurement object. It is not an equivalent acceleration of the former whole-clock protocol, retrospective repair, tolerance relaxation, or measured algorithm speedup. R112/R113/R114 source qualification, missing clocks, cap failures and original pairs remain unchanged.

The main quantity is `T_produce=t_produce-t0`. Arm1 starts at its actual wrapper OS creation, including imports and admission; subsequent arms start at the actual recorded pre-admission boundary. No clock resets at H handoff. Public/group administration is separately recorded without favorable retroactive allocation.

Cold P keeps original cold behavior.

`t_produce` is taken only after these outputs are written and closed. It is neither Gurobi Runtime, last MIPSOL time nor last log time. The subsequent timestamp receipt proves that boundary and does not cover its own future write. Receipt management is charged to the subsequent verification interval and campaign outer clock.

The only work relocated is Python work after the original native has stopped: independent replay of already committed own historical fleets, full saved-model/type/Start/journal/domain/cover/return corroboration, source claim rejection and audit closeout. None supplies production decisions. The original online C++ checks and all supervisor checks used while a child is alive remain inside production. Postverification can confirm/revoke existing claims and fleets; it cannot generate/redecode a new solution, start an LP/MIP, improve UB/LB, reenter native or backfill certificates at `t_produce`. The known analytic nonnegative floor0 is fixed beforehand. Positive proof/domain obligations must already exist in sealed production evidence.

Only the unchanged complete R114 mathematical/model/physical/domain/return checks, after production, permit a formal endpoint. Proven bad calls lose every bound/OPTIMAL/INFEASIBLE/pruning/positive-certificate dependency, including later smaller bounds. Other calls retain independent qualification. Nested H/LP/MIP/audit timers are diagnostic and never added again. The total receipt is written afterwards as campaign management; no receipt claims to cover future writes or future review. Four-arm cohort reconciliation, report pairing, public export/restoration and further review have group/campaign or engineering clocks and cannot be assigned to a closed arm. Later qualification revocation records its actual time without changing old receipts.

An actual producer overcap, missing mandatory production output or real execution/resource/identity fault still blocks; native tail/serialization cannot be removed and an earlier incumbent cannot replace the final clock. Ordinary positive/negative performance, backend not exposed and symmetrically isolated proven claim rejection do not cancel later methods.
