\section{From inventory prices to physical fleets}
The stable Gini-interval tailored branch-and-cut method (GI-TBC/B, S2/ms2)
retains complete improving-domain coverage, valid cutoffs and a scope-aware
proof ledger. E+ retains RN16384 and the inherited proof-only consumer.
The present study is an isolated primal resource experiment. Neither a
resource witness nor an inner-domain bound is silently consumed by the
complete exact engine.

With the original binary64 input coefficients, write $r_i=c_iY_i$,
$S=\sum_i r_i$, and, for $S>0$,
\[
 G(Y)=\frac{\sum_{i<j}|r_i-r_j|}{nS},\quad
 H(Y)=\sum_i\omega_i|r_i-1|,\quad J(Y)=G(Y)+H(Y).
\]
The coefficient $\omega_i$ includes $\lambda$. The repaired independent zero
branch is retained; the two studied RN scopes have a qualified positive
stock denominator and $\gamma_L=0$. All comparisons bind the same inventory
supports, original improvement scope, weights and numerical contract.

Let $D_I$ denote the inherited integer inventory relaxation. For RX35,
$D_F$ removes precisely the original 26 vectors excluded by the R157
necessary-resource certificates; for NB35, $D_F=D_I$. Let $D_R(T)$ project
unique legal station service, eligible integer ownership, per-car balances,
return capacity, complete handling and necessary tours of at most three
stations. Let $D_P(T)$ be the physical fleet projection. In the inherited
qualified contract,
\[
 D_P(T)\subseteq D_R(T)\subseteq D_F\subseteq D_I,
 \qquad F_I\le F_F\le F_R\le F_P.
\]
The middle inclusion uses the R157 resource exclusions and R158's actual
C++ duration/budget qualification, rather than physical impossibility alone.
Later finite exclusions do not redefine $D_F$.

An independently verified integer resource point gives $U_R$ and hence
\[
 0\le F_R-F_I\le U_R-L_I,\qquad
 \eta_R=\frac{U_R-L_I}{U_P^{\rm ref}-L_I}.
\]
The reference $U_P^{\rm ref}$ is held fixed for each role. This scalar
interval indicator is not realized complete-algorithm gap reduction.
If $D_R\subseteq D_C\subseteq D_I$, then $F_C\le F_R\le U_R$.
This statement does not cap the joint benefit of cuts in a full routing
relaxation $Q$: the resource point may have no compatible full lift into $Q$.
Likewise, $U_P-U_R$ is a witness-price difference, not a lower bound on
$F_P-F_R$.

\section{A bounded simultaneous inner domain}
For vehicle $k$, let $P_k$ and $D_k$ be cumulative pickups and deliveries.
Empty departure gives return $R_k=P_k-D_k$, with $0\le R_k\le Q_k$.
Including return unloading,
\[
 t_pP_k+t_d(D_k+R_k)=(t_p+t_d)P_k.
\]
Cumulative $P_k$ may exceed $Q_k$. Only each single station operation and
the return are bounded by vehicle capacity in the resource model; physical
prefix loads are checked separately.

The short fixed-owner reference optimizes original $J$ over full supported
quantities on the initial disjoint owner sets $A_k$. It allows zero service
and direction changes, while initially unassigned stations retain $Y_i=b_i$.
A full-$A_k$ three-stop budget remains conservative after zeroing a station.
This is an inner restriction, not exhaustive fixed-owner quantity search.
The original RN centering/scale and one-sided envelope are retained.

The joint mechanism releases all supported quantities and eligible owners
on eight stations at once. Every outside quantity and nonzero owner remains
fixed, with its full pickup, delivery and return contribution included.
Conditional active-subset budgets are rebuilt for every vehicle. Activation,
zero service, direction changes, cross-car ownership and unequal-net
quantity/ownership compensation are permitted without inheriting an
uncovered old active-set budget.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Score station $i$ by its best unconstrained single-stock reduction in
true $J$, multiplied by one plus current owner handling pressure.
\item Choose the step-ranked anchor, then seven stations by that score plus
anchor-weighted geometric proximity; break ties by station index.
\item Solve the joint original-J subproblem with a complete feasible Start,
Seed 0, Threads 1 and at most 512 nodes. Retain strict true-J improvements and
read them back independently in the complete original resource domain.
\item Stop after 12 blocks or the ordinary whole-method deadline. No
nondecreasing intermediate move or time-triggered algorithm switch is used.
\end{enumerate}

Both roles use the same rule and capacities. The short references use 2048
nodes each. The declared domain can coordinate eight changes beyond one
single-stock change, owner move/swap or pair transfer. This is not a
reachability theorem against every sequence of historical moves. Trace
evidence distinguishes allowed freedoms from those actually exercised.
Every subproblem lies inside $D_R$. Its feasible point can give a global
resource upper; its bound, optimum or failure remains local. None enters
the global proof ledger.

The new repeated model construction, block selection, callback and completion
are C++. The standalone tool loads an explicitly exported, unchanged
qualified RN objective; this offline boundary is not represented as a
fully integrated production candidate. With $s=\sum_i|\mathcal Y_i|$, the
new model has $O(Ms+n^2)$ columns and $O(Mn^3+s+n^2)$ rows. A node limit
does not bound presolve time or establish polynomial complexity.

For fixed inventory and ownership, the load after a visited subset $S$ is
$\sum_{i\in S}(b_i-Y_i)$. The classical $(S,\mathrm{last})$ dynamic program
therefore covers every elementary order while retaining only
$0\le\mathrm{load}(S)\le Q_k$. A second, unloaded recurrence isolates the
tour contribution. Both use exact additions of the supplied dyadic travel
coefficients at scale $2^{53}$ in 128-bit integers, with a common capacity
of 16777216 subset/last slots. An exhausted complete recurrence gives a
fixed-combination minimum; insufficient capacity gives UNKNOWN.
Work is $O(m^2 2^m)$ and storage $O(m2^m)$.

These are inherited classical tools, not new theory. Their precision is
bound to the supplied discrete coefficients and acceptance contract, not
all symbolic Euclidean calculations. A loaded-tour minimum at an old $Y$
is not transported as a necessary lower for variable new quantities. No
new variable-Y tour cut or complete-$Q$ extension ceiling is claimed.
