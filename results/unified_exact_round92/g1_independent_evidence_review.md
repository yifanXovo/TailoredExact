## Physical validity boundary found after G1

`Instance` stores `T`, pickup/drop times and distances as `double`; CLI uses `std::stod` for the times. The physical `Evaluator` uses the raw values, while `writeExpr` serializes any individual duration coefficient within `1e-12` of one as exactly one. This can make the canonical duration model slightly **stricter** than the physical time law. Let `δ=2^-42`. Take two stations, one vehicle of capacity 2, all directed travel 0, station initial/capacity `(2,1)`, positive targets `(1,1)` and weights, `T=2−δ`, and `pickup_time=drop_time=(1−δ)/2`. All numbers are exactly representable binary fractions, inputs are nonnegative and metric, and the route `0→1→0` picking 2 bikes is physically feasible: it returns loaded and takes `2−2δ<T`; the untouched second station leaves a positive objective denominator. The raw service coefficient is `c=1−δ`, but the writer emits `c=1`. Round92 consequently computes `B=floor((2−δ)/1)=1`, so its `P<=B a` row excludes this physically feasible route with `P=2,a=1`. The old canonical duration row also excludes it, since `2>2−δ`: this does **not** refute validity relative to the frozen canonical model. It does refute a general claim that a canonical-valid Round92 row is automatically valid for every feasible original-physical route. The physical verifier's existing `1e-7` time acceptance is a further boundary, not a justification for using the tighter row.

A uniform repair can keep the original duration writer and its tolerances untouched while deriving the new row from **lower** per-arc coefficients `min(raw physical d, emitted d)` and a lower service coefficient `min(exact dyadic pickup+drop, emitted c)`, then applying the same directed shortest-path lower enclosure and safe quotient upper-floor. This proves the row for the union of exact raw-physical and canonical integer feasible routes, provided `c_lower>0` and all supported arcs match. If `c_lower=0`, no handling row follows from time. To cover the actual `Evaluator` acceptance rule as well, the numerator must also conservatively include its existing `1e-7` time tolerance and a rigorous enclosure for possible downward floating evaluation error; simply taking per-coefficient minima with the old `T` does not prove that stronger numerical statement. A corrected proof key and ledger must identify the lower-coefficient source. No per-instance disabling or adjustable slack is warranted.

**Hold R92 native G2 and comparative G3 performance admission until the uniform physical-validity contract is resolved and statically reviewed.** This finding does not affect the frozen Round90 D6 tail binary or the G1 structural receipts.
