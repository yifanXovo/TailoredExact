# Default-off scalar proof formulation contract

The original R118 physical problem and full paper specification are unchanged. For service stations only, Y=b+sum(d-p), r=Y/D, F=G_true+lambda sum(w abs(r-1)). The zero ratio-sum convention is G_true=0. Empty departure, unique nonzero one-way integer service, inventory capacities and all prefix/return loads in [0,Q] remain. Loaded return is legal and paid; travel is Parser Euclidean/1.5 and duration is travel+(pickup_seconds+drop_seconds)*total_pickup. Q is simultaneous capacity, not cumulative pickup. min_ratio is not a stock floor. M-B retains continuous p/d, integer Y/load, binary state/assignment/direction and original A/B. H retains Seed20260626 and its full 24+1 closure/exchange procedure; every formal arm pays its own H.

A returned optimal child LP C_j asserts F>=ell_j only for original physical points with G_true in its closed half I_j and F<=c_j, conditional on the original numerical/embedding contract. No unreturned, unknown, feasible-objective or local-node value is admitted. Splitting the full half into that set and its cutoff complement proves beta_j=min(ell_j,c_j). In particular ell=1 under cutoff .5 cannot exclude a physical F=.6 outside that cutoff. The containing-domain direction is necessary: a bound on [1,2] cannot prove the same bound on [0,2]. Different source cutoffs are individually complemented.

The production floor is max(0,down(min(ell,c)-1e-7)). The fixed inherited 1e-7 evidence margin is not tuned. Zero is justified by objective nonnegativity, and cannot repair a source counterexample. Directed coefficient/endpoint guards then supplement that margin. These are floating-point numerical certificates, not rational solver proofs. Exact rational checks of stored coefficients verify only their conservative serialization and algebra. The final 1e-7 original certification policy is unchanged.

For original root [a,b], actual m=a+(b-a)/2 and h binary, D1 adds:

```text
g >= a+(m-a)h
g <= m+(b-m)h
f >= beta0+(beta1-beta0)h
```

D0 keeps the first two rows and replaces the third by f>=min(beta0,beta1). Both add exactly one column and three ordinary linear rows, without priorities, indicators or arbitrary big M. f is the complete native linear objective, including ObjCon. The actual writer extracts that complete expression and moves its actual constant into the row RHS. Native API readback checks every coefficient against the native objective, including h's zero objective coefficient.

For each physical feasible point in the original root and its model's non-strict cutoff, select g=G_true. Extend Y and product auxiliaries through the original exact VD-P block: state selected at Y_i, state_g at that state equal to g, zprod=gY, and the unchanged complete route/resource representation. Assign h=0 when G_true<=m, otherwise h=1. The returned proof or its cutoff complement gives F>=beta_j, so the new rows hold and f=F. Every strict improver remains under the own original root cutoff; its outside-cutoff complement cannot improve U. Thus the complete original improving problem's projection and optimum are preserved. Original strengthening only claims coverage under its valid stock domains/cutoff, not objective-preserving embeddings of points excluded by the old cutoff.

Conversely the new model is a restriction of the original integer model. Its unchanged integer-to-physical projection and f>=F implication still hold. Every original UB is separately physically replayed; an auxiliary/native objective is never substituted for physical F. Epigraph g need not equal physical G in arbitrary integer completions. New rows may remove a virtual-g completion of a still legal physical fleet while retaining its exact-g extension. This is why the rows are a pre-Optimize formulation and not asserted global user cuts. No incumbent crossing adds g<=m in a callback, and no lazy-constraint substitute is used.

In the theoretical improvement domain f<=u<beta1, h=1 is impossible, hence g<=m; the opposite case is symmetric. A physical own-U update need not be a native incumbent or cutoff. Actual native objective, physical F and sampled h are separately recorded. Presence of h, a threshold crossing, or h=0 in one incumbent does not establish native fixing or branching. Those internal events are unknown unless an actual supported observation establishes them.

Relaxing h to [0,1], for beta1>=beta0 and a<=g<=b the scalar projection has f>=beta0 and f>=beta0+(beta1-beta0)(g-m)/(b-m). If beta0>beta1 it has f>=beta1 and f>=beta1+(beta0-beta1)(m-g)/(m-a). These are the lower envelopes of the two scalar interval epigraphs' convex hull; they are not the full BRP integer hull or the strength of duplicated complete child F0 blocks. Degenerate partitions are rejected; the denominators occur only in this explanation, never the production rows.

Serialization separately guards base and difference sums. For the actual coefficients, lower-row endpoints at h=0/1 must be <=a/m, upper endpoints >=m/b, and objective endpoints (including actual ObjCon) <=qualified beta0/beta1 (the common floor for D0). Full decimal Fraction audits and actual native binary64 readback both check these sums. Small outward extensions are retained in the audit; there is no inward epsilon gap at m. Numeric validity of coefficients is separate from reliability of the underlying LP evidence.

Only the original root pair is registered, once, before that root's first integer Optimize. An incomplete pair, an already requested integer root, or original AM splitting directly to children leaves the complete run unexposed: no later repair, forced root job or added probe. Once root installation occurs, all subsequent original MIP requests retain the same extension or carry it into a separately identified original domain/epoch generation whose interval is contained in [a,b]. Old LP/model files remain immutable and old scoped bounds keep their source identity. Target and terminal are original distinct requests; no whole-search-tree preservation or single-Optimize complete algorithm is claimed.

Own Start maps all original exact physical/auxiliary columns before h, with the fixed left midpoint tie. Actual native bounds/types, all complete rows and full objective are checked, submitted and read back. All work, callback observations, installation, writes, mapping and native cleanup consume the original producer deadline. Source contradiction revokes the pair and every dependent native bound/closure; a legal own fleet does not restore exactness. A fatal correctness callback is distinct from a valid-proof/U crossing; the latter has no new stop path.
