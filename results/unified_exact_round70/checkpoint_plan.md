# Within-run checkpoints, declared before D6 candidate results

Use300 and600 seconds from each already declared D6/D7 run. This is a read-only
analysis plan: no new optimizer launch, repeated search, changed source,
deadline or performance threshold. The600 point is the completed endpoint.

The qualified Round69 parser conservatively shifts buffered bound callbacks
to account for missing setup and an unobserved tail. This may understate early
progress differently across arms; intermediate bounds must not be promoted to
precise speed ranks. Final endpoints are unaffected and alone enter pairs.csv.

DS publishes candidate events at generation0, which is never treated as a
generation clock. Link a complete retained initial route by its canonical hash
to a published and physically verified event, and date it conservatively by
the decoded_descent_complete process event after the synchronous callback.
The original HGA arms retain their generation-completion upper-bound clock.
If no qualified early link exists, wait until exact-tree initialization.

No final native route is backdated. Intermediate P incumbents remain native
telemetry because complete historical vectors were not retained; their UB
column is not an independently replayed physical certificate. Candidate early
UBs use retained initial routes only, even if native telemetry later reports
a better incumbent. Preserve these scope differences beside the numbers.
