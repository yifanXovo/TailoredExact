# Frozen diagnostic and confirmation rule

This rule was written before any Round 38 medium-cap diagnostic result existed.
The unchanged smoke-frozen `G2-A` executable advances to every row of the
12-case frozen development panel, paired serially against the protected
`C6-HGA-FULL` reference at a 480-second process cap.

The diagnostic stage is admissible only if every checksum, arm-contract,
coverage, lifecycle, monotonic-bound, feasibility, artifact, and atomic
completion gate passes, with zero false strict certificates and zero strict
certificate regressions.  The Round 37 stable positive witness (panel ordinal
8) must not become a final-gap regression, and the Round 37 stable negative
witness (ordinal 10) must not regain a final-gap regression.  Timing, node
count, solver work, labels, and instance identifiers are descriptive only and
cannot select a refinement.

A bounded confirmation may be frozen only for: (a) the two stable witnesses
when ordinal 8 retains a positive final-gap effect and ordinal 10 remains
non-regressive, or (b) a new coherent structural stratum containing at least
two panel rows with the same sign and the same frontier mechanism.  Any worst
diagnostic regression must accompany the selected positive rows.  Confirmation
uses an unchanged executable and policy, a 900-second process cap, and at most
four pairs.

Promotion additionally requires a direct, deterministic global-frontier
mechanism: completed initial bounds, a unique controlling cell, a strict next
frontier, and at least one audited refinement whose child bound reaches that
frontier.  Pure effects from rejected speculative lookahead, runtime, work, or
path perturbation cannot justify promotion.  Therefore clean computational
results can still end in an explicit no-promotion decision.
