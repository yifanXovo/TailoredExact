# Post-freeze research-document updates

The three experiment manifests freeze executable code, commands, matrices, the
pre-result hypothesis state, and the protocol before their respective runs.
After all experiments closed, two non-executable research documents changed:

- `hypothesis_register.json` moved G1 from the frozen pre-result status to the
  final evidence conclusion and left G2 explicitly untested.
- `research_protocol.md` received a rendering-only repair for corrupted dash and
  range characters. Its substantive policy, stage ranges, metrics, gates, and
  no-promotion rule are unchanged.

The original SHA-256 values remain immutable in every stage freeze. This file
records why those two live conclusion documents no longer match their pre-run
hashes. No executable source, runner, command, matrix, panel, or candidate rule
changed after the corresponding freeze.
