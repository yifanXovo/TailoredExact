## Evidence and frozen interpretation

Keep 57 checkpoint rows and 57 pair comparisons: F1 at 30/60/120; F2 at
60/120/180/300; F5/F6 at 300/600/1200/1800/2400/3600 seconds. There are
19 protection rows and 12 final endpoints. Use each run's own physical U,
qualified complete-domain L and original source/build identity. Availability
is max(payload close, completed observation); final extraction is available
only after normal exit. No cross-run combined performance endpoints.

Missing physical U is a legitimate open outcome of the full original model:
keep U/gap/relative gap null and retain only qualified L. Distinguish no U
from a verified final-only route with no observed callback witness. Verify
that route independently against all earlier bounds and do not fabricate an
earlier witness. Restricted cutoff domains still require a physical witness.
Offline reader tests must cover these cases before native launch; historical
R85 producers/readers are unchanged. Any finalization/receipt error remains
an evidence failure, not a favorable performance row.

Frozen R85 practical rules apply without outcome-based changes. Both-certified
small (V<=12, both walls<60): material >2s AND20%, severe >5s AND50%.
Other certified: material >10s AND15%, severe >30s AND50%.
Both open: material gap difference >.001 AND10%; severe loss >.01 AND50%.
Certificate gain/loss is separate. Relative gap=(U-L)/abs(U), undefined for
missing/zero U. Keep signed tiny gaps; reject contradictions beyond the
original 1e-7 certificate/physical check tolerance. These are descriptive
acceptance rules, not statistical significance or algorithm parameters.

Compare P first; report K1 losses jointly with P gains and retained meaningful
K1/P advantage. Retention below .5 is a serious disclosed loss, not an isolated
K1-only veto. Do not average away key nonzero large losses, hide certificate
losses or equate a mixed U/L gap gain with uniformly improved primal search.
All generated roles remain in denominators, including missing U and open runs.

Overall acceptance uses this confirmation together with the predefined
historical repair/protection scope, not just a favorable subset. It requires
the unified admissible method, repaired small/nonzero CitiBike defects,
credible gains on the nonzero large roles with no unresolved major P loss,
and basic preservation of important K1 advantages. One weak/inconclusive
role cannot be declared solved by an average. If serious new losses appear,
analyze their primal/proof origin and revise or redirect the core method;
these outcomes then become development, and a revised method needs another
unadapted check. Do not repeat neutral-neighborhood micro-tuning indefinitely.
If new large samples prove easy, disclose insufficient hard-confirmation
coverage rather than silently adding or replacing samples. Any additional
stage needs a separate finite admission before outcomes.
