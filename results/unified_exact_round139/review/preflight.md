## Mathematical contract

The four inherited stock inequalities are valid for every exact-product integer
completion on its declared interval, including an auxiliary epigraph G above the
true Gini. Thus any of 00/11/10/01 preserves integer feasibility. The ideal
same-domain LP lower bound is nondecreasing when adding them; this is not a
performance theorem. For a cutoff-restricted solve, the regional unconditional
bound is min(ell,c), or c after qualified restricted infeasibility. The existing
NativeEvidenceJournal::evaluateNativeLocalBound first checks same-domain witness
contradictions and preconditions and then returns min(b,c). Keep raw native
observations, qualified regional floors and externally merged bounds distinct.
Only parent-to-contained-child inheritance is automatic; child-to-parent needs
complete coverage. The scheduler retains the maximum of applicable floors.

The actual adaptive-mass evaluator first handles logical infeasibility, then
tests the minimum child bound against parent+tolerance, and only then compares
eta*mu against tau. Increasing parent and child bounds does not order their
differences or normalized score. The K20 target-to-exact-close interpretation
matches this code. Neither the formula nor this one observation is new general
controller theory.
