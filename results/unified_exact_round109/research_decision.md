# Round109 research decision, before any new draw or measurement

R108 supports only SELECT_MB_FOR_BROAD_EVALUATION for the original R100 M-B.
Its 21 complete same-PE/DLL formal arms yielded six WIN and one TIE versus cold
P-GRB; S12/B24/L48/N36 yielded three WIN and no LOSS. S12's 28.1777-second gain
remains below the frozen 30-second materiality floor. F2, F5 and N36 losses
versus ENS remain part of the record, with F5 severe. ENS remains the default.

The remaining candidate question concerns a wider prospective geographic panel
and a finite second native Seed check. R108's largest formal V was 50; its H100
qualification input actually has 20 stations and fleet [13,21]. This round adds
four genuine V100/M8 roles, alongside four V20/M2 and four V50/M4 roles. The
public R57 table supplies paired coordinates and capacity from the same source
row. Compact/regional geography, shortage/balanced/surplus stock, homogeneous
or equal-total heterogeneous fleets and physical horizons are fixed in twelve
rows before generation. These rows cover a scenario panel; they do not isolate
factor effects and are not twelve independent geographic samples or a new city.
The three Seed1 repeats address limited native seed sensitivity, not ENS seed
robustness or general random-seed stability.

The sole algorithm identity is the original full ENS framework plus R100
m-binary state service. Production functions, mathematical rows, p/d implied
integrality, startup, AM0.08, interval coverage, terminal calls and original
physical evaluation are inherited. R108's L48 had two actual parent splits,
including one AM split and at most two relevant active leaves; NEXT, terminal,
right-domain LP infeasibility and the high-G complement jointly establish its
certificate. Saved open labels alone do not create new unresolved obligations.
This evidence does not reopen R107 assignment/STRUCT.

R108 C2 gains and F5/N36 losses primarily concern own UB. In F5 the M-B/ENS
gap difference was about .03104011 despite a slightly stronger M-B LB; ENS had
a safe observed .333465758 witness by 559.325 seconds, better than M-B's final
.345021271. Endpoint decomposition and recorded availability distinguish
primal/proof deficits without identifying p/d, A/B or native branching causes.
This round uses existing observations to examine whether such deficits recur,
with no added solver call for explanation and no mechanism implementation.

All 42 valid normal arms must finish regardless of intermediate signs. The
frozen support rules require at least six main M-B/P WIN, at most two LOSS, no
severe P regression, a WIN in each size and geometry, and the prescribed Seed
conditions. BROAD_PANEL_SUPPORT closes this frozen ENS-MB as a paper research
candidate with limited empirical claims, without switching defaults or claiming
a completed paper benchmark. BROAD_PANEL_NOT_SUPPORTED denies this promotion,
distinguishing regression, Seed sensitivity and insufficient/easy evidence;
it leaves the R108 selection record intact. BLOCKED is reserved for unresolved
identity, correctness, coverage, completeness or actual environmental failure.
Neither outcome authorizes new variants, draws, seeds or automatic next-round
optimization. New prospective evidence and candidate judgment are the added
research work; old mathematical properties and audit tooling are not new
algorithmic contributions.
