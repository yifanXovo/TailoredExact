# NC20 post-primal endpoint: independent evidence assessment

Disposition: `ACCEPT_QUANTIFIED_UNRESOLVED_STAGE_ENDPOINT`, subject to the
planned paid final audit. The registered updated-level experiment,
support transfer, and conditional final primal refresh are sealed. They
support a bounded, unresolved goal E3 stage conclusion. They do not
establish the complete hull to 1e-5, exhaust the method family, or close
the whole Round153 goal. The final scientific and delivery review remains
separate.

This independent nonimplementer review read the five small iteration
records and price summaries, plans, the support-LP result, three closed
outer receipts, the refresh return, and bounded beginning/end excerpts
of the large retained result files. It did not import a solver, read a
model, Optimize, replay, enumerate or requalify the 54,210-column pool,
compile, or execute another validation program. Earlier long-price,
near-optimal, primal-probe and screen observations are those already
bound in the prior independent reviews. The actual full-chain audit has
not yet been read as PASS. Full B/J outcomes were not used to select this
research endpoint.

## Relation to the earlier evidence and stage judgment

The earlier long price of `level_zero_continued/iter00008` already made
its own direct certificate nearly as strong as its true-stock ceiling.
That ceiling, approximately `0.1833229562`, is only about 1.11e-6 above
the present lower, below the 1e-5 research target. Its large remaining
full-hull gap cannot be addressed by blindly extending that same price.
This ceiling statement is specific to that certificate and does not
exclude support complementarity in principle.

The subsequent near-optimal zero-center phase tested a different
direction-selection regime, found columns, and improved the numerical
upper. The 106-sweep primal probe then grew the pool to 54,187 stocks
with only about 3e-13 further upper movement. The paid finite screen
showed that the enlarged pool genuinely contradicted the latest old
near-optimal dual: true price upper fell to about 0.03955552, yielding
negative reduced cost about -0.00450893 and a fixed ceiling near
0.18544776. This justified selecting a fresh midpoint dual against the
new pool instead of treating the old direction as a proof candidate
arbitrarily close to the old RMP upper.

The present experiment actually performs that change. It still finds
true negative columns, but five completed full prices leave wide price
intervals and no material whole-hull movement. The free-Q support replay
and final RMP refresh remove the ambiguity that useful new supports or
the last three columns were simply never assessed. The result locates
an unresolved pricing/dual-extension barrier with a tested primal-column
explanation showing little effect. It does not prove that useful stock
mixtures or better dual directions do not exist.

For the limited E3 stage claim, this is adequate targeted deepening:
the accumulated valid lower is materially above the same-domain
reference; the older almost-closed direction has little direct room;
new near-optimal, expanded-pool, and refreshed-level evidence identifies
the remaining computational difficulty. The endpoint is not justified
merely because five iterations or a local time allowance ended. The
last price was still discovering columns, and its Native log shows a
bound still advancing in its final minute, so neither exhaustion nor
stagnation should be claimed.

No additional expensive run is required to make that limited unresolved
conclusion honest. A longer new midpoint price still has potential, but
the current data do not establish an inexpensive route to a materially
tighter whole-hull interval, and its fixed ceiling cannot close the
interval by itself. Repeating the old nearly closed direction, another
large local primal pool, or an automatic cap grid lacks a new specific
decision rationale. This review does not authorize such a continuation
or infer a negative family result from the tested costs.

The next required step is the registered finite audit and overall
mathematical/data/scope/cost review. If the final claim is instead that
the NC20 complete hull is known to 1e-5, the current evidence fails that
claim and further research would be needed. Production helpers and the
completed full panel remain separate and unchanged.
