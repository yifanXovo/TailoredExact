3.1    A fractional ownership obstruction
An integer stock can be difficult for the resource model even when its inventory variables already
have an exact normalized lift. The following elementary construction separates inventory integrality
from vehicle ownership integrality. It concerns the explicit bare LPs, before additional integer-valid
cuts or branching.

Proposition 3. Let an integer stock have an inventory LP lift in the hard level. Suppose there
are M ≥ 2 vehicles, every nonzero service is individually eligible for every vehicle, and its total
pickups/drops P, D satisfy

                           0 ≤ (P − D)/M ≤ min Qk ,          P ≤ (M − 1)U.

vehicle extends that stock to a feasible LP point of both ideal resource expressions, including all their
rounded and forbidden-subset rows.

instantiation at price J(Y ) can upper-bound the original-price optimum of those fixed bare LPs. It
cannot upper-bound FR , and additional integer-valid cuts can remove it. In particular, survival of
one impossible stock in the LP does not establish a strict gap between the global inventory and
resource minima. Actual binary-matrix coefficients and RHS values require a separate saved-point
check before an exact numerical LP witness is reported.

5    Evidence consumption and the complete method
A whole-level resource lower is a bound for the original inventory scope and can enter the GI-TBC
ledger only with that scope and its coverage qualification. A local family proof cannot close all
low-objective inventories. A physical route can update U only after the unchanged original-time,
station, vehicle, operation and prefix-load checks. A relaxed-time assignment, a log objective or an
unfinished callback point does not substitute for either certificate.
    Any complete E+R candidate must pay for its own inventory pricing, resource-level computation,
route construction and verification. Offline stocks, old assignments and proofs are not free starts for
a nominal fresh arm. A uniform rule may use current mathematical bounds and fixed node/iteration
capacities; it may not dispatch on instance names, historical wins or a timer switch. A worthwhile
structural finding need not become a production mechanism. Stable GI-TBC exposition and the
maintained paper continue independently of whether this research branch produces such a candidate.
