## Actual interface defect found and corrected

The original refinement writer copied its entire prior result. Starting
from a primal-only result would therefore carry
`primal_discovery_only=True`, `no_new_lower_claim=True` and its historical
primal_sweeps into a new lower-producing refinement. The finite audit's
primal branch would then expect a primal plan and unchanged lower/supports;
the result would fail that audit and be mislabeled in reports.

Before execution, the root corrected both refine and support_close
outputs to set `primal_discovery_only=False`, `no_new_lower_claim=False`
and `primal_sweeps=[]`, and to retain ancestor evidence through
prior_results. The corrected lines were read. They describe the actual
new phase; they do not remove, rewrite or disqualify the saved ancestor
primal evidence. The unchanged coupled entry already constructs a fresh
result dictionary and does not have this inheritance defect. No new
Native qualification is needed for this output-type correction.

## Interpretation after the sealed returns

The registered material-gain thresholds remain approximately 0.00044665
for K35 and 0.00066350 for NC20. They guide the continuation decision;
smaller valid improvements are retained, and failure to meet a threshold
is not an invalid-bound or convergence rule. Record both interval movement
and actual cost, as well as remaining price brackets, true candidate
prices, corrections, RMP-minus-D_K and useful column response.

If K35's longer price finds a genuinely new negative column, retain it.
A support-LP plateau alone would then not settle its possible benefit to
the restricted primal upper; one appropriately qualified RMP reoptimization
may become the next justified cheap check. It is not automatically added
by this plan. If the price instead approaches a_K with a qualified lower,
the resulting interval directly answers the intended proof question.

For NC20, the new level may or may not yield more tractable or stronger
directions. Neither a selected midpoint nor a large number of added
columns is success without a qualified interval improvement or a clearer
quantified bottleneck. At the returns, reassess actual progress and residual
potential under goal E3. Do not automatically extend both caps, repeat a
grid, or treat time/no-column stops as full-hull convergence or a family
negative. This finite plan adds meaningful targeted evidence to the
already completed alternatives rather than presuming a desired outcome.
