## New-only carrier

`scripts/round163_carrier.py package` packages current-round evidence,
qualification failures and new authoring/readback sources. Indexed ZIP members
retain exact local bytes, including original models, journals, routes, source
and price qualifications, actual argv, clocks and fee receipts. Round163 Git
attributes also preserve actual protocol/source bytes. Old raw archives,
commercial DLLs, licenses and credentials are excluded. The reused measured
PE stays at its verified local path and is not duplicated as new evidence.
Its old build/dependency identity is pinned by the candidate manifest.

Use a Windows host with the identified compatible project runtime and a valid
local Gurobi license for an actual semantic or performance rerun. A source
rebuild is a new binary identity until qualified; a filename is not proof of
equivalence. Existing absolute paths in measured receipts are provenance, not
instance dispatch rules. New paths may be generically rebound with their own
actual identities, without changing the frozen algorithm.

## Bounded public recovery

After the normal science commit/push, `round163_carrier.py restore COMMIT`
downloads only the new ZIPs anonymously to an isolated directory under
`E:/codes/ExactEBRP-work/restore`. It checks every archive and indexed member
against saved byte counts and SHA256. No old archive, compilation, Optimize,
commercial runtime or license is fetched. The actual completion and scope are
reported in `public_restore_receipt.json`; absence of that receipt is not PASS.
This check establishes new evidence byte/member recovery, not an independent
licensed Windows semantic or performance rerun.

`main_table_update.json` separately records actual writes to the two identified
legacy master CSV copies. Their original GB18030 prefix and 28-column header
(including two empty source-header cells) are preserved. The named 28-column
increment and the complete 18-arm B/E+/P table support inspection and merging.
No E+ result occupies the GI-TBC/B master column.
