# Directed primary-source reading, 15 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai)

This is the internal reading record. Public bibliography entries contain
bibliographic facts, with the inherited search/reading notes moved to
`bibliography_reading_notes.md`. The primary PDFs used for local reading
stay under the work-root temporary directory and are not republished.
`literature_versions.json` identifies their actual downloaded bytes.

* Huchette and Vielma, author PDF `6148.pdf`, 45 pages, PDF pp9-11, section
  3.2: LogE can be ideal and compact while variable branching leaves weak
  dual information. The discussion defines projected-volume/strengthened-
  proportion behavior and sharp/hereditary distinctions. This is established
  formulation/branching context; it neither predicts the project's Native
  trajectory nor supplies a new priority claim. DOI 10.1287/opre.2019.1973
  belongs to Operations Research 71(5), 1835-1856 (2023), online 2022.
  [Actual author version](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/6148.pdf).
* Behle, 2007 thesis, 98 pages, PDF pp38-40 (printed pp30-32), section
  4.1 and Theorem 4.1: the BDD polytope is the projection of conserved unit
  path flow and level equations. Its proof uses convex combinations of path
  vertices. D167's path projection uses this standard mechanism; the shared
  G-weighted flow and service-piece costs specify the project joint object.
  [Actual thesis](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/alumni/d1/2019/behle/publications/behle_thesis.pdf).
* Balas, Annals of Operations Research 140, 125-161 (2005), 37-page PDF,
  pp16-18 (printed pp140-142), section 5 and Theorem 5.1: a union's closed
  convex hull has the standard disaggregated perspective extension. The
  two-endpoint network decomposition here is a specialization of that tool,
  with bounded finite path objects, not a new disjunctive theorem.
  [Actual article](https://www3.diism.unisi.it/~agnetis/projlift.pdf).
* Muldoon, Adams and Sherali, Operations Research Letters 41(1), 32-39
  (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.orl.2012.10.005: publisher-indexed abstract and
  introduction/excerpts were accessible through web search; a direct page
  fetch returned HTTP403. They characterize bounded lexicographic binary
  sets and apply the hulls to ideal base-2 expansions. Full subscription
  text was not obtained. No theorem-by-theorem full-paper novelty exclusion
  is claimed. [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167637712001290).
* van Hoeve, 28-page 2024 author tutorial, PDF pp14-15, section 6's opening
  polyhedral view and conserved-flow equations (10)-(14); the web-extracted
  network passage was also read. Exact diagrams can generally be large;
  the bounded-width size here follows from this particular interval
  automaton, not a generic promise of logarithmic decision diagrams.
  [Actual tutorial](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/vanhoeve/papers/DD_TutORial.pdf).
