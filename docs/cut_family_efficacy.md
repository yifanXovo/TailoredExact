# Static-family efficacy audit

Round 55 audited every active F0-CLEAN row family at frozen fixed-interval roots.  Activity, dual participation, bound contribution, construction cost, and controlled removal were recorded separately; a family was not called useless merely because one LP gave it zero dual.

The pair and triple support-duration cover families were the only large active families with zero observed activity, zero nonzero duals, and zero isolated root-bound contribution across the 162-diagnostic census.  Their costs were nevertheless measured causally:

- triple covers contributed 216,919 generated rows; removing them gave shifted Work geometric mean 0.869 in the diagnostic removal census;
- pair covers contributed 21,294 generated rows; removing them gave shifted Work geometric mean 0.934.

The pre-pilot sparse gate selected no removal because the frozen evidence rule required simultaneous exactness, multi-role negligible contribution, material cost, and a uniform live-candidate justification.  One later controlled revision, SF-R1, removed only triple support-duration covers while retaining pair covers.  Its D1 build census removed exactly 660 triple rows (3,764 to 3,104 F0 rows) and preserved all 198 pair rows.  In D1--D14 live development it preserved the aggregate certificate count but lost the D12 certificate and produced severe regressions on D1 and D12.  SF-R1 was therefore rejected, and the permitted second sparse revision was declined.

Accordingly, K1-AM-SF retains both pair and triple support-duration covers.  Round 55's sparse-management classification is `no_additional_family_removed`.  The audit is a bounded empirical result, not a proof that these families can never help.
