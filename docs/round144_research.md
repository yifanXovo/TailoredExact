# Round144: RN information in original-variable rows

RN-P is a separate, default-off research extension of stable R137 GI-TBC.
The binary flag defaults off; the dedicated research launcher explicitly
selects RN-P unless `--projection off` is requested.
It obtains one production-capped continuous RN source after the run's own H
and initial root are known. A full RN-block dual combination is projected
through the finite extension-variable box and transported with outward
coefficient compensation. Formal LP/MIP models retain their original
columns, objective, penalty and complete Start; they carry at most one
selector/G row and no persistent RN columns.

The [mathematical note](../results/unified_exact_round144/mathematical_analysis.md)
states validity separately from exact same-root optimal-value preservation.
The latter requires an exact optimal dual, the same original domain and the
same objective; it is the standard principle already studied in R104.
Neither complete RN projection nor equal bounds throughout the tree follows.
R104's negative complete-search results remain relevant prior evidence.

The [LP report](../results/unified_exact_round144/LP_report.md) measures actual
compiled/transported rows, including finite-precision loss. The
[complete-method report](../results/unified_exact_round144/final_report.md),
[full-precision results](../results/unified_exact_round144/reports/full.json)
and [adoption decision](../results/unified_exact_round144/candidate_decision.json)
retain all entered same-PE positions and their complete clocks. Each run
obtains its own H; no saved research point or bound is injected. Gurobi
Seed=0, H seed=20260626, one thread and serial timing apply unless an
explicitly recorded confirmation says otherwise.

`config/gi_tbc_rnp_round144.json`. It prints a dry-run plan by default;

control. Its standard arguments are those of `scripts/paper_s2.py`.

authorities. A local rebuild has a new binary identity and must not be
