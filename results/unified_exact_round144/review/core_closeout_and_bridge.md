# Complete-core judgment and one attribution bridge

The actual mixed four-role core reasonably supports **research-only**
retention, while preserving the positive computational evidence. This is a
joint result judgment, not a mechanical veto from one bound or one role.

K35 gives a material verified U/gap improvement with a weaker supported L.
RX20 proves the same objective somewhat faster. RB35 has an important U/gap
regression. K20 gives the same U, a slightly weaker L and about 1.14 seconds
more production time. Its measured verification saving of 225.05 seconds and
complete-cost saving of 223.91 seconds remain actual results and are not
waived or normalized away. Because K20 submits no PC and retains the same
formal matrices, that saving does not establish structural search acceleration.
The evidence establishes real information retention and some finite role
benefits, but not a convincing uniform stable-method upgrade across resource
and proof-clock roles.

The one full-window K35 persistent-RN bridge is independently admitted in
`bridge_admission.json`. It uses the same V5 PE, source, physical/objective
input, Seed0, own H and 2400-second cap as the existing K35 OFF and RN-P runs.
Only the explicit RN flag and output destination differ from the normalized
OFF launch. The RN reader checks all old R143 finite-capped RN matrices and
Native audits; the RN-P source-proof exclusion is inactive for this method.

This bridge can change an identified scientific conclusion: whether the
projected method's operational benefit also holds against persistent RN,
rather than only OFF. Compare U, supported L, gap and complete clocks jointly.
Even a favorable bridge does not erase RB35 or uniquely identify column count
as the cause. An unfavorable bridge limits the representation-cost gain while
leaving the mathematically verified compact root information intact.

No additional control is needed simply to make a candid research-only
decision. If stable adoption is genuinely reopened, a predeclared matched
Seed1 group on the role controlling that decision could change confidence in
the observed benefit/regression; its opposite result must be capable of
changing the action, and all Seed0 evidence remains. A paid-floor comparison
is useful only if a concrete complete-search structural-versus-scalar
attribution or adoption choice depends on it. The already executed local
same-objective certificate is exact within its declared continuous block;
it does not claim full-routing or full-search floor dominance. No control
grid, new implementation or extra role expansion follows automatically.
