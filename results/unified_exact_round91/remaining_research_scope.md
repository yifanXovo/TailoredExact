## Material open questions, with finite gates

**LP-G (the only current end-to-end algorithm candidate).** G3 covered 16 valid paired runs and showed C2's seed-0 certified benefit, D3 near equality, and mixed censored D7/U6/F5/F6 gaps. The fixed C2 seeds 0/1/2 favored LP-G twice but seed 2 slowed 11.16%; this meets only the preregistered priority rule, not an all-seed or P-GRB advantage (`round90/lp_g_g3_decision.md`, `c2_repeat_decision.md`). First accept the already running F2/D6 protection receipts and any preregistered severe-stop outcome. If an identity/certificate fault occurs, classify and repair evidence before performance inference. If a serious protected-case regression or lost certificate persists under the existing rule, park LP-G without a new split variant. If it survives, finish only the remaining fixed G4 protection roles; treat open 1200 s endpoints as censored. There is no justification yet for broad G5, mainline default, or a claim of better P-GRB convergence. The LP-G point can remove the current parent fractional product point in some cases, but neither AM's conditional gap-contraction lemma nor a smaller root-LP bound guarantees runtime improvement.

**C1 depth 8.** Existing retained leaves are shallow; R87 hard-stopped U6/F5 expose at least depth-2 candidate LPs but incomplete final trees, and Round90 C2 admitted two splits. No complete evidence shows depth 8 actually blocking proof. Removing it is not needed for a retained ENS-C or LP-G method. Midpoint plus positive minimum width would give finite geometry under the stated assumptions, but LP-G interior points may be arbitrarily unbalanced and infeasible-child contractions bypass the AM score; the conditional AM contraction lemma does **not** license unbounded depth. Keep and disclose depth 8. A depth≥9 coverage/terminal fixture is a finite gate **only if** a future observed run hits the cap and there is a separately justified reason to remove it; no depth experiment for checklist completion (`round88/split_mathematics.md`, `c1_depth_cap_observation.md`; `round90/am_gap_contraction_note.md`).

**C3 zero-gain shortcut.** The valid theorem needs a full optimal parent vector feasible for an identified child model with all current rows, columns, bounds, cutoff and incumbent epoch; scalar `G`, zero aggregate product error or an AM `no_strict_child_improvement` label does not supply that witness. The default controller usually retains only scalar `G`, and constructing/checking a child may consume much of the intended saving (`round88/split_next_gate_review.md`). Keep the original full child-LP/AM/native decision. If LP-G passes protection and the saved ledger shows a material repeated zero-gain cost, allow at most one preselected same-state feasibility/cost audit with a genuinely available full primal and child artifact; require a complete verified mapping and measured cost less than the avoided LP. Otherwise close C3 as unproven and do not add a shortcut. A zero immediate LP gain would still not prove no later B&B benefit.

**D1/D2.** D1 has neither an observed multi-case terminal-MIP bottleneck attributable to a particular fractional inventory threshold nor a demonstrated production interface for that exact disjunction; Gurobi variable priority is not custom branching. The finite entry gate, if a candidate survives G4, is one retained fractional-node witness plus a precise API/coverage contract distinct from the old R50 priority negatives; absent either, park without a screen. D2's historical IR1/MC4/VD-J/ARC/QPLUS/LOG/time-flow negatives remain, and the anticipated B1/B2 coupling premise has not yielded a qualified full method. No old-model parameter matrix should be reopened. Only a new, independently shown defect in a surviving candidate at an exactly reconstructed R55 harmed state would supply a finite D2 hypothesis; otherwise D2 is closed for this cycle. Do not relabel a root bound change as certificate-time gain.

Existing R87 D6/F2 wins and Round90 LP-G/G3 gaps are useful development context, not a complete contemporary all-panel P comparison. If that standard cannot be met within finite admitted runs, report the objective as unresolved rather than manufacturing a winner from certified-only averages, historical UBs or censored times. The required compiled, visually checked paper-style LaTeX/PDF and reproduction package precede any mainline promotion or broad long campaign; neither has been supplied by the present mechanism screens.

The write-up must describe the **actual** algorithm, not an idealized one: 24 random plus one constructive start, finite descent with top-8/top-6 compound-neighborhood restriction, AM score 0.08, positive minimum G width `10⁻⁴`, depth 8 and exact terminal MIP/coverage; `compact_bc_support_cut_max_subsets=50000` actively truncates rank≤3 rows and can make vehicle order matter. These fixed counts are model/heuristic choices rather than prohibited Time/Work service slices, but their scope and costs cannot be omitted. `external_gini_tree_max_observed_depth` is currently a stale zero for the paper controller: C2 seed-1 LP-G actually admitted depth-2 splits. Distinguish selected split point, completed child LPs, AM proposal, atomic adoption and terminal-parent MIP *request* from an actually certified close.

Mathematical validity, solver-numerical LP/UB evidence and original physical certification need separate labels. A verified incumbent cutoff is an original feasible U, while an `OPTIMAL` imported LP objective is a solver-numerical L under residual checks, not a rational dual proof or a finished MIP. Preserve all startup/build/model/export/LP/native/verification/failed-attempt costs; no internal speed-dependent dispatch, instance-specific algorithm patch, hidden time slice, or comparison of only surviving certified arms. D3's parser prefers coordinates (rebuilding travel at speed 1.5) over serialized distances; actual canonical row coefficients govern any proposed travel cut. Novelty of OT, routing/time and branching components has not been established by a failed search or by these local proofs; cite prior art accurately if any of those mechanisms enters a final contribution claim.

The frozen `round91_handling_real_diagnostic.py` already performs the following **before its first Optimize** on each signed case: checks script/prereg/LP/input and historical archive SHA, Round90 gate/binary/ENS-C launch, initial verified U and L0 ledger status, imports and reads the old LP, validates its G interval/objective/cutoff, original depot/visit/F0/quantity/time rows and domains, computes dyadic directed shortest paths and conservative B/C coefficients, rereads/relaxes A and checks original matrix identity. The real interpreter/Gurobi import and license are also exercised on this path before Optimize; a duplicate zero-Optimize LP replay adds little. What this self-audit does **not** cover is external launch identity and the launch-to-exit **process-tree** 120 s watchdog (including interpreter startup), actual A/B/C solver returns, B/C added-row read-back (which occurs only after A), complete primal/row/bound residuals, status-dependent numeric L, partial receipts and total cost. Root can bind and inspect those in the separately admitted actual diagnostic; it need not commission duplicate LP audits or mirror tests. A malformed identity must fail before Optimize, and a B/C late audit fault consumes recorded A cost but yields no comparison. That diagnostic remains optional relevance evidence, not a reason to delay the active LP-G protection batch.
