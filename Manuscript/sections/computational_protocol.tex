\section{Computational Protocol}
Round 54 froze contracts, panels, promotion gates, source identity, and process
caps before inspecting new performance. Ordinary processes were capped at
3,600 seconds; the predeclared 7,200-second V50 extension could open only after
successful K1 integration. Every official fixed-interval comparator and
candidate row used the same frozen Gurobi-enabled executable, one thread, seed
zero, automatic presolve, zero MIP gaps, and complete process accounting.

The paper-preset identity audit used major, strong-control, V10 easy-negative,
numerical-endpoint, V20, and V50 sentinels. All six have identical deterministic
settings, controller actions, intervals, backend identity, objective, bound,
and certificate class. Where model construction was reached, exported model
files are byte-identical. The bounded V20/V50 runs remained in heuristic
startup and produced no fixed-interval model file; this is reported as
semantic command/controller evidence, not fabricated model evidence.

The inventory--route offline census covered 34 frozen states and both IR1 and
IR2. Both variants were valid on every state, showed a strict violation in 33
structural roles, and improved the final root bound in 25 states. IR2 produced
no strict final-closure gain over IR1, so the least expansive IR1 entered the
live fixed-interval gate; the condition to open IR3 was absent.

Stage A compared F0--CLEAN and IR1 on D1--D14 at 300 seconds: 28 complete rows
and 14 matched pairs. F0 certified 11/14 and IR1 9/14, with zero false
certificates. IR1 gained one material hard-state improvement (D4), but lost
the D1 and D13 certificates. Its shifted Work geometric-mean ratio was
1.089432; aggregate GI worsened from 0.498349 to 0.623423. The frozen Stage-B
gate therefore failed. Fixed-interval Stage B, confirmation and long checks,
full K1 integration, and the sealed V12/V20/V50 generalization runs were not
opened. Empty schema-valid ledgers and opening decisions record this outcome.

The evidence supports K1--AM--SF as the stable mainline and classifies
inventory--route strengthening as a bounded negative result. It does not
support K1--AM--SF--IR or a universal paper-algorithm claim.

\subsection{Round 55 station-state chain}
Round 55 began with an engineering audit rather than a formulation race. A
stale incumbent-epoch cache identity was repaired, a corrected stable baseline
was frozen, and 11 paired semantic sentinels plus D1--D14 requalification were
completed before any candidate result opened. Both clean official builds pass
33/33 tests; the dedicated station-state target contains 58 checks.

The frozen root census contains 108 formulation rows over 27 states. MC4
strictly strengthens 24 roots and VD--J supplies additional joint strength;
VD--P matches the MC4 root bounds through its extended representation. Model
size, root Work, memory, and total MIP behavior are reported independently.
All four valid formulations entered the 120-second pilot. MC4 failed a severe
regression guard. VD--P and VD--J entered D1--D14 development; VD--J lost a
certificate and was rejected, while VD--P advanced.

The only controlled sparse revision, SF--R1, removed the triple-duration
family uniformly while retaining pair covers. It preserved the aggregate
certificate count but lost D12 and was severe on D1 and D12, so it was
rejected. VD--P then completed 18 C1--C9 confirmation rows and 38 extension
rows against F0--CLEAN. It gained one and two certificates, respectively,
with zero loss, zero severe regression, and zero false certificate. The
shifted Work geometric-mean ratios were 0.840 and 0.664; aggregate GI ratios
were 0.817 and 0.506. Penalty-cover and interaction performance stages did not
open because their preconditions were false; empty ledgers record those gate
outcomes rather than fabricated experiments.

The key-long fixed-interval panel contained 22 rows over 11 states and retained
the VD--P leader. Full controller integration then entered 34 physical rows at
1,800 seconds and 10 conditionally extended rows at 3,600 seconds. Across the
17 effective pairs, VD--P produced 14 certificates versus 12 for F0--CLEAN,
with shifted-Work geometric-mean ratio 0.5692 and aggregate GI ratio 0.6091.
There were no false certificates and V20/V50 behavior was nonworse. However,
the predeclared major witness suffered one severe regression, so the full K1
gate failed. Materially changed split actions occurred only on three other
inputs; the witness failure was therefore not attributed to the split rule.
No split revision, sealed P--GRB panel, or strong-result expansion was opened.
VD--P remains a default-off research candidate and the paper preset remains
K1--AM--SF with F0--CLEAN.
