# General revision: one constructive seed in the same finite decoded descent

Declared after the complete, audited ten-run initial tranche. Initial JI is
preserved as a separate default-off research preset and negative/mixed evidence.
Its D6 loss does not justify an instance-specific selector. The revision uses
one JI-generated route order as an additional seed for the same finite DS-X
decoded descent, allowing existing visit orders and decoded quantities to change.

The proposed single startup procedure constructs and verifies JI, retains its
physical witness, initializes the unchanged24 fixed-seed random individuals,
appends one constructive individual, and fully descends each using the existing
DS-X neighborhood. No evolutionary loop or internal seconds/Work cutoff is
introduced. The one extra seed corresponds to a distinct mathematical
construction, not a renamed runtime allocation. The final best verified startup
witness feeds one complete unchanged VD-S/AM proof; no independent exact
algorithms or their per-instance best endpoints are combined.

JI sequences preserve constructed visit order and vehicle assignments. Any
unvisited stations are appended deterministically to a tail so that the
chromosome includes every station exactly once; full decoding decides service.
The exact tail rule must be documented and tested before freezing execution.
The original physical JI witness is retained even if greedy decoding of its
chromosome is worse. Empty/invalid/duplicate seeds fail explicit admission.
The24 original random chromosomes and their complete descent traces must be
tested unchanged when the whole startup completes, excluding elapsed times
and separately scoped best-observer events. A shared cache may change cost,
not mathematical decode values. This gives a startup-quality retention check,
not a proof of faster end-to-end performance or deadline dominance.

Resource extension: implement an isolated new preset and meaningful seed
integrity/cache/exhaustion/deadline/native CLI tests. One fresh isolated full
qualification batch plus required repair attempts, with all native calls and
costs retained. Qualifier versions v4/v5 are reserved for this revision; v1-v3
remain immutable. Following a passing batch, at most10 additional fresh UB-only
diagnoses: D3/C2/D4/D6/D7 times fresh DS-X/new seeded descent,30s per process,
maximum300s, zero Optimize. This is a separate output and identity, not a
replacement of the initial tranche. No new full-performance/long/confirmation
budget is granted here. The six originally declared native persistence tests
remain unused, rather than being reallocated silently.

Require actual constructive-seed descent beyond a mere best-of-two UB merge.
If the extra seed cannot improve the relevant quality/cost tradeoff, retain
that negative evidence and reassess direct quantity/vehicle-assignment repair.
The first priority is the known D6/D7 startup tradeoff; full proof benefit and
deadline persistence must still be established before a long comparison.

Before execution, tail completion is fixed as follows: scan unvisited station
IDs ascending; append each to the vehicle minimizing existing constructed
physical duration plus the append-travel delta, updating this travel proxy.
Ties choose the lowest vehicle index. This is only a permutation-completion
proxy; no unverified service quantity or physical UB is assigned to a tail.
All24 random individuals are initialized before the constructive individual
is appended, so its creation consumes no random draws. Native tests must
verify all25 seed terminal neighborhoods and retain the original24 traces.
