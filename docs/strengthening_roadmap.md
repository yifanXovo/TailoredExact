## Round 55 disposition and one next step

IR1/IR2 were often violated and strengthened 25/34 frozen root states, so Track
A was not an offline-negative idea. However, selected IR1 lost two F0
certificates, worsened shifted Work geometric mean to 1.089432, and worsened
aggregate GI. Track A therefore stops as a bounded negative result.

Round 55 proved VD-P and VD-J exact and found strong fixed-interval evidence
for VD-P. In full K1 integration, VD-P improved certificates from 12 to 14 and
reduced aggregate Work/GI, but severely regressed the predeclared major
witness. The failure had no materially changed split action on that witness,
so neither a split revision nor sealed P-GRB qualification was authorized.

**The single recommended next step is a parameter-free formulation-lifecycle
study of the major witness:** isolate why VD-P's larger exact model is costly
there while preserving the formulation and controller unchanged. Do not tune
by instance, reopen the split threshold, or open sealed P-GRB until a uniform
causal mechanism passes a newly frozen development and K1 gate.
