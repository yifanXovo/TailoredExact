# Finite historical attribution used by the manuscript

These are source-directed readings of the published R112/R113/R115 final reports at R123 head fca82e8737183aceb9d5f8950d2f2322393ae6fd. No old carrier or campaign was restored.

R112's paid-start P-S control independently runs the complete current H and maps its own full fleet into the original compact formulation without changing that numeric matrix. The original task ended BLOCKED, with 12 qualified observations, one interrupted arm and seven unstarted positions over five roles. The six completed warm/control startup comparisons had matching full 25-seed semantics, actions, capacity-assigned fleets and inventories. Such controls permit local backend comparisons, but not a complete five-role attribution claim.

The completed old F2 observations include tailored nonzero proof completion while both P/P-S remain open. Cold P had an early eventual-target fleet, so that comparison is not evidence that every gain comes from earlier primal discovery. The P-S comparison also contains a physical endpoint difference. On L48, P-S certifies before both tailored methods, which are nonsevere losses against P-S. C2 retains an ENS/cold-P severe loss. Observed zero AM splits in some cases cannot support an explanation based on realized splitting.

R113's geographic extension preserves a rejected cold-P positive bound contradicted by an independently valid own-zero fleet. The cold record remains unqualified. Warm same-H checks and local physical results survive only in their admitted scope; a valid own zero does not repair dependent native pruning.

R115 completes the remaining fixed-role attribution with measurement-v2 production/verification separation. Its descriptive five-role layer has ENS/P-S W/W/L/W/L and M-B/P-S W/W/L/T/L: both eligible5/5 MIXED. It combines older whole-clock and newer production-clock evidence only as within-role classifications; no pooled exact speedup or unified-clock benchmark is supported. The new G100 P-S owns an independent zero certificate, while damaged native positive-bound sources are rejected for all affected methods. Independent floor zero and legal physical witnesses are not counted as repaired positive native proof. This old V100 role is a historical boundary, not a newly authorized experimental scope; R124 generates and solves nothing, with normal future scope still V≤50.

These controls establish that H, native proof and representation effects must be distinguished. They do not isolate every row's causal effect, prove that an unexposed feature helped, or guarantee M-B dominates a paid-start control. Historical PE/source/window identities differ from the R122/R123 panel. The main paper therefore reports the matched current panel as its primary evidence and uses these old findings only to delimit attribution.
