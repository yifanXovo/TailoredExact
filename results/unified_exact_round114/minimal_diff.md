# Round114 implementation boundary

Only new Round114 helpers, qualification scripts, offline reader and evidence are authored. The 206 production source bytes, inherited PE and Gurobi DLL are frozen. All older helpers and original evidence bytes remain unchanged. This is a necessary research audit and closeout protocol change, not a display-only change or an algorithm change.

`round114_claims.py` assigns contradictions to their actual source call and verified original/restricted domain, invalidates every native proof dependency of a damaged call, and establishes independent own-UB/floor0 or own-zero endpoints with complete mandatory evidence. It sorts the full claim and witness sets before reconciliation. Its last-MIP missing-return exception requires the known failure latch, directly verified same-domain counterexample, committed own exact-zero fleet, complete independently corroborated return and all mandatory fields.

`round114_audit.py` first verifies committed identities, actual native activity, real returns, cleanup, physical fleet and nonnegative original objective. A precisely proven bound claim rejection has a distinct exception type. Its model/scope/Start and independent cover geometry checks remain mandatory; damaged native cover numbers remain raw diagnostics. Unknown journal, identity/model/physical, I/O or lifecycle failures still stop the group.

`round114_ps.py` retains the inherited full Start construction/submission/readback audit and adds the four contradictory-native-field checks on every supported zero-Optimize branch. These checks are finite fixtures and do not claim actual native cancellation.

`round114_campaign.py` extracts the original R90 supervisor with four explicit replacements: native-end receipt, a typed rejection exit, postaudit receipt and continuation only after a typed rejection's full independent audit. Original affinity, memory/disk cadence, kill/wait, hard stop and reserve remain. Every actual current arm persists own independent evidence, rejection/cohort records, metrics, publication receipt and closeout digests before taking its true whole timestamp. Future offline reconciliation cannot rewrite that clock. A wrapper fee closes only after its actual operating-system exit.

`qualify_round114.py` bills finite real-entry and source-extracted mock tests, exact saved-matrix counterexample replay and the limited signed inherited return contracts. `qualify_round114_post.py` bills actual H100 raw/clock reconstruction. Synthetic evidence stays separate from the two actual fixed CLI runs. Failed qualification slots are retained and never refunded.

`read_round114.py` requires an explicit root, reconstructs current own endpoints and final cohort qualification, preserves the historical 15 measurements/21 evaluable pairs and missing G50 cold P, reports six new pairs, full same-H semantics, source qualification, native counts and observations. No historical missing clock or result is created. Qualification CLI are excluded from performance comparisons.

The new helper closure is recorded in candidate/campaign identities. Only the output roots, method order authorized for this round, necessary audit helpers and evidence layer differ from the inherited G100 command semantics. No H, model, parameter, callback, online UB/cache/cutoff or solver termination change is introduced.
