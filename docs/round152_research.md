# Round152: hard-resource integer objective epigraphs

The [stage report](../results/unified_exact_round152/final_report.md) completes
an offline study of the original Gini-plus-penalty objective on necessary
hard integer inventory domains. Stable R137 GI-TBC remains the paper method.

The [mathematical analysis](../results/unified_exact_round152/mathematical_analysis.md)
and [independent exact theorem](../results/unified_exact_round152/review/exact_small_theorem.md)
separate AP's fixed expected penalty from the remaining common-distribution
Gini epigraph. A heterogeneous complete inventory block has exact same-marginal
gap 53/1080 and a separate global-integer-floor gap 1/480 beyond the declared
AP/GD/stock/count/common-factor reference. The R133 control is closed by that
reference; no full-route or all-historical-hull dominance is inferred.

Three complete saved domains retain all variables free. New results confront
AP, relevant old count/Gini information and then RN. N-B35 has a real scalar
floor increment, K35's residual rows add no value beyond old RN, and N-C20 has
a small residual-support increment. Eight-row loops expose repeated marginal
movement. Bin MILP plus a qualified complement-DP lift and an exact positive-
denominator integer RN oracle give computable support rows, with explicit
LB/UB, zero-state, nonminimal-completion and scope qualification.
