## What the support replay and price improvement establish

The free-Q replay keeps all original variables free, the original Q SHA,
zero projection rows, and all 82 accumulated objective supports. Its
all-supports LP interval is
`[0.2828596948801659, 0.2828596948801739]`. The recorded numerical primal
constraint residual is about 1.82e-12 and bounds residual is zero. These
are the inherited numerical LP qualification, not a rational primal
certificate. Its near-zero width solves this finite-support relaxation;
its upper is **not** an upper for the complete objective hull. The latter
continues to use the retained RMP upper `0.28732616330431715`.

The new price lower improves by about 0.00911157, removing roughly 46%
of the old direction's unresolved price width. It is real directional
progress. Nevertheless, this C_Q/a_K correction is still about
0.00636552 below the accumulated complete-hull lower, and the stronger
82nd support does not materially improve the combined free-Q value.
The latest bound change of about 1e-15 is numerical, not a new research
gain.

For this fixed certificate only, the price lower would need to exceed
approximately 0.07640395 just to beat the current accumulated lower.
The predeclared material-gain screen, about 0.00044665, would require
approximately 0.07685059. Approaching a 1e-4 hull width through this
direction would require approximately 0.08077041. These are rounded
diagnostic thresholds, not additional lower certificates.

The fixed-certificate ceiling remains `0.2873260533067223`, almost the
current RMP upper. Thus neither the expanded true pool nor this price
test rules out substantial further benefit. A ceiling is only an upper
limit on what this saved C_Q/a_K certificate could prove if priced
tightly; it is not an attained lower and does not bound the potential
of other directions or interacting supports.

The sealed Native log also rules out calling the price numerically
stagnant: its displayed best bound advances from about 0.11825 at 300 s,
to 0.12261 at 600 s, 0.12377 at 900 s, and 0.1246511041 at return. These
are values of the same shifted/scaled Native objective within this run,
not directly comparable absolute values to another run's original-price
bound. Progress slows but remains positive through the final interval.
Extrapolating these rounded log rates is not a reliable time-to-proof
forecast and is not a reason to declare future work useless.

## Stage judgment and narrowly scoped next action

The new test completes the specific diagnostic left open after the
large primal probe: the enlarged pool did not contradict the saved
near-optimal K35 dual, yet a fourfold fixed-price allowance still leaves
a large proof gap and produces neither a new column nor a useful
combined-support improvement. Together with the earlier changed dual
selection, old-direction long price, targeted partition/CDF/graph
comparisons, all-support transfer, and 64 completed primal sweeps, this
is substantive targeted deepening rather than stopping at an arbitrary
old iteration count. The bottleneck is now quantified at this tested
direction and cost; the evidence does not require that every domain
reach the same small gap.

Accordingly, no additional expensive K35 run is necessary to support
the limited E3 conclusion: a positive complete-domain lower improvement
has been established, while the remaining complete-hull value is open
because extending a promising near-optimal dual is expensive. There is
no newly observed negative-price stock or materially changed upper that
would require a fresh midpoint level pass, and no demonstrated fast
alternative from the completed comparisons. Automatically extending
the cap or repeating a large primal neighborhood would not answer a
newly identified question. This is a stage decision, not proof that
another longer price or different mechanism could not help.

The registered conditional refresh should be skipped for K35: the final
price added no stock that has not already participated in the free
complete-Q RMP. A refresh of the same pool is not required merely because
the last support was strengthened. Record that concrete skip reason.

The necessary next actions are the already planned final audit of the
Q residual/price/support/primal witness chain and the final data and
scope review after NC20 closes. If a later claim instead requires the
K35 full-hull value to 1e-4, the present record cannot support it. The
promising saved beta-near-one direction and its explicit threshold above
are an identified continuation problem, but this review does not
authorize another run or precommit to spending the remaining capacity.
Any future continuation must retain all existing bounds and pools,
identify a concrete decision it can change, and be serially metered.
