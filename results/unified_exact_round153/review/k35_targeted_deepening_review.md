# K35 price-depth and near-optimal zero-center review

Decision: **ACCEPT_TARGETED_RESEARCH_DESIGN** for the two proposed follow-ups.
This is an independent nonimplementer review using source inspection and
mathematical reasoning only. No Native call, compilation, budget calculation
or heavy evidence replay was performed. The primary reports that both
segmented comparisons and their paid-bound overlay failed to improve the
retained monolithic price lower; those unsuccessful attempts and their fees
remain part of the evidence. The current NC20 continuation is not treated
as a completed study.

## One full-range price with a longer Native allowance

Using the already reviewed refinement driver with `parts=1` covers the
entire exact positive-S interval with one conditional model and keeps the
legal zero state at the parent. The rational coverage, reciprocal bounds,
one-hot transportation and full-domain recombination proofs do not depend
on there being four or sixteen pieces. The single child receives the whole
3600-second Native allowance. This is an increase in the search allowance
for one fixed full price, not a new restricted inventory domain. Existing
legal stocks can be used as warm candidates, and the previously valid
same-direction lower must be retained by a maximum.

The unchanged source is the same saved `K-35-U_price_deep/iter00003` Q dual,
with its exact beta/d, C_Q and true-column a_K identity checks. The resulting
master lower is still `C_Q + min(a_K, L_price)`, used once. All construction,
readback, search, output and necessary checking costs count in the new serial
outer receipt; a 3600-second Native cap is not a declaration of a 3600-second
total experiment cost.

The scientific rationale is finite and quantitative. The primary's current
true-price upper implies that this fixed direction can at most raise its
corrected lower to about .283685, versus the accumulated .28182616. That
approximately .00186 potential gain is material compared with the present
.00567 interval. It cannot by itself close the full-hull interval to the
requested accuracy: even that maximum would leave about .00381 below the
current .28749181 upper. A tighter price upper can reduce this potential
ceiling. Report the new price interval, actual increment and cost rather
than treating a longer run as evidence of eventual closure. The more narrow
S models' failure does not prove the unpartitioned long search unhelpful.

## Near-optimal zero-center column generation

The proposed `zero_pool` mode selects an approximately optimal current RMP
dual with a band no larger than 1e-7, and minimizes the existing zero-center
L1 objective over its linking multipliers and beta. It adds no potential
box. This addresses a different question from the previous level selector:
whether forcing the price direction near the current RMP dual objective
finds useful columns or certified lower bounds that a lower intermediate
level did not expose. AP-centered near-optimal stagnation does not establish
that the zero-center choice behaves the same way; neither choice guarantees
improvement.

The design keeps Q, the full inventory domain, pre-column Q-only bounds,
the true min_K/a_K correction and the numerical RMP primal checks unchanged.
The selector's band/objective is never a full-hull lower. Its selected Pi,
or the recorded basic-dual fallback, must still pass the exact Q residual
certificate and guarded complete-domain pricing. Adding all returned true
legal pool columns is valid regardless of their candidate reduced costs;
every old RMP primal extends by zero new lambda weights. All prior columns,
supports, qualified lower bounds and numerical primal uppers remain available.

The concrete mode must set all four intended choices together:

- stabilization enabled;
- level continuation disabled;
- all legal pool columns enabled;
- center exactly zero.

At the reviewed source version `zero_pool` was not yet in the stabilization
or pool allowlists, and the zero-center branch recognized only a
`level_zero` prefix. Those dispatches must all be updated; the prefix alone
would incorrectly select the AP center for the proposed new name. The price
factory remains the original monolithic complete-domain Oracle. Record the
actual band, selected center, fallback status and new/negative column counts.
This limited driver change needs static interface confirmation before use,
not a new Native small-domain qualification of an unchanged formulation.

The 7200-second offline window with 300-second full prices is accepted as
one targeted continuation using all accumulated evidence. Stop normally on
a qualified interval meeting the target. No fresh legal column with a still
wide interval is an unresolved checkpoint, including when the capped price
has not excluded negative reduced cost; it is not proof that the complete
hull has converged. Do not repeat an unchanged selected dual indefinitely
or reinterpret the per-run limit as the scientific stopping criterion.

Both steps must run serially after the current active timed job closes or
reaches its authorized normal checkpoint. Retain the ledger capacity for
the admitted complete panel and its necessary verification. These changes
are offline research only and leave the frozen production chain untouched.
