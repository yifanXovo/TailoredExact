three-arm D7 campaign must close, pass its independent audits and be published
as an independent draft PR first. K1's final endpoint remains pending when
this note is written.

If the complete screen preserves the important K1 advantage while materially
improving P, freeze the present BDS-C implementation. The next useful question
is its full-method behavior on the exposed small/nonzero roles, not another
neighborhood expansion. A bounded next stage can reuse the qualified binary
and run fresh P/BDS-C/K1-R on E7, S12, N12 at common 120-second caps and D3,
C2, D4 at common 300-second caps: 18 serial arms, at most 3780 process seconds,
plus six zero-Optimize compact reference exports. No new build/test is needed
unless source identity changes. A new plan must freeze the order, call ceiling,
endpoint rules and handling of any early mathematical zero certificate.

The six roles distinguish original small regression, CitiBike surplus/shortage,
multi-vehicle nonzero proof difficulty and compact short-T behavior. Their
previously exposed status is explicit. Identical startup on D3/C2/D4 does not
replace full timing. K1 is a fresh reference; no historical fastest-point veto
is introduced. The existing practical thresholds stay unchanged.

If that panel supports the frozen candidate, the next consequential allocation
is matched common 3600-second P/BDS-C/K1-R on D6 and D7, with within-run observed
checkpoints. These longer arms are not admitted here. D6 is especially useful:
its new starter improves only modestly, and its prior K1 advantage concerns a
long nonzero proof. A single positive exposed D7 screen cannot establish stable
medium/large superiority. Later confirmation must include unadapted, structurally
diverse nonzero medium/large roles and account for related geographic samples.

to frame a paid-starter core comparison. No result automatically selects an
