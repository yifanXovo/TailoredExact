# Physical realization of low-price inventories

The Round157 study connects original inventory-objective certificates to
the physical fleet question. It preserves free vehicle attribution and
route order, original heterogeneous capacities, empty departures, legal
loaded returns and depot unloading. Exact coefficient classes retain
spatial rearrangement without changing the original objective.

On RX35 the complete six-member best-price family is excluded by a
qualified necessary resource time lower above the original6300-second
limit. The original fixed-inventory fleet solve remains UNKNOWN by its
own return; the stronger family exclusion also excludes that inventory.
NB35's singleton remains UNKNOWN. These family statements alone do not
exclude a whole objective level. A full-domain resource diagnostic stalls
at the inherited global floor and is preserved as incomplete, with all
fees charged. The preselected second RX35 family is also excluded, for 26
member vectors in total. One finite batch and one RN16384 repricing cost
200.85 outer seconds and retain the old global lower. The selected complete
objective level remains unresolved. There is no demonstrated useful,
plausibly affordable complete E+R mechanism; the conditional panel is not
started. The report records the finite propagation result and stopping
decision.
