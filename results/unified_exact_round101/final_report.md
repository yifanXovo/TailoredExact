## Mathematical and actual-model result

Empty departure and nonnegative loaded return imply total pickup at least total delivery; subset time therefore needs a safe travel lower bound plus `c*max(pickup thresholds,delivery thresholds)`. Q limits a single operation, never cumulative pickup. Extra suppliers, receivers, visits, arbitrary legal orders and loaded return remain possible.
