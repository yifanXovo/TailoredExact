# Stop quantity-only continuation and close this diagnostic expansion

Seven initially nonattaining saved B skeletons all have a qualified
F_order lower above their fixed same-input archive target. C35 Seed0's
F_owner is also excluded. The two archive-self-target parents are HIT
without solving, and the one largest C35 eligible domain is UNKNOWN.
There is no nontrivial order HIT and no qualified exclusion/HIT separation
that identifies one sufficient released structure. This is an informative
negative localization, not a new online algorithm.

**Decision: recommend no new complete-method mechanism from this round.**
Keep uniform GI-TBC/B, C166/D167 default off. Close automatic extension
of these conditional MIPs: no extra time, Seed, inputs, changed target,
alternative parent or broad eligible rerun. Preserve the unresolved
eligible interval and return the current single manuscript to substantive
author/coauthor assessment. No Round169 or pilot is launched.

## What the evidence settles

The old complete fixed-order family is insufficient to reach the closed
archive target for every one of the seven nontrivial saved skeletons.
This is stronger than R164's failure to embed one particular target stock
vector: the lower covers every legal stock vector, sign flip, zero
deletion and compressed travel path at fixed owner/order. It does not
assert that quantities can never improve. X35 Seeds1/2 improve legally
by about.00126410/.000396874 while still failing the target.

For C35 Seed0, free reordering at original owner is still insufficient:
safe L=.09339833077738849 exceeds theta=.09331944374779841. Thus any
target-reaching full fleet must change at least one old served owner or
serve an old ineligible station. The original P fleet does the latter at
station18. Its existence does not prove that18 must be served in every
target-reaching fleet. Eligible is UNKNOWN with final safe interval
[0,.11512886899258191], using the feasible owner witness as its upper.
The Native eligible bound is retained raw, but its incumbent residual
7.9123969e-7 fails the pre-frozen1e-7 gate, so only nonnegativity supplies
the safe lower. No threshold or solver tolerance is revised afterward.

The C35 owner witness improves F by.0144375206594, changes six stocks,
deletes station35 and reorders two cars without a sign flip or owner
change. It is still above target. This describes one feasible witness;
it does not prove which action caused a Native search event or that
reordering alone suffices at the unchanged quantities.

## Why no renamed old mechanism is proposed

R76 quantity/insertion closure, R78 balanced relocation and R83 equal-net
exchange are finite actions. R96 already solves the entire order family,
including all service signs and zero deletions. Reimplementing its oracle
or paying it on another own-state is not by itself a new mechanism.
R97 demonstrates mixed whole-run outcomes for late closure/feedback.
R159's restricted quantity/owner resource results do not certify full
fleets. R160 already jointly releases quantities, owner and order on
up to8 stations with bounded broken ports/outside runs, and its complete
comparison is adverse. Simply enlarging a block or swapping in this
larger MIP supplies no new algorithmic principle or paid benefit evidence.

The present evidence rejects a quantity-only repair of the stated target
deficits and narrows C35 to an owner-or-service-set disjunction. It does
not choose one sufficient action inside that disjunction. The broadest
conditional MIP has low information efficiency for this remaining choice:
300s yields neither a target witness nor a qualified nontrivial lower.
The smaller family exclusions were fast and useful; we do not call the
whole diagnostic uninformative. We close its *expansion* because no new
sufficient-action evidence or distinct own-state rule supports a pilot.

The precise unresolved, falsifiable claim is: a fleet confined to C35
Seed0's old E can attain theta when ownership is released. Current
UNKNOWN neither establishes nor refutes it. Any future proposed own-state
method would first need a genuinely distinct structural construction,
an argument beyond the old finite neighborhoods, and a separately frozen
limited complete comparison paying startup, construction, checking and
solver search together. It cannot use archive tau or P routes as an
online oracle. Those are admission conditions for a new idea, not a
scheduled follow-up or a reason to repeat the present MIPs.
