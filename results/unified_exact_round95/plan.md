## Exact integer load domain

For an original route prefix with load L and vehicle capacity Q_k, let sigma_a/sigma_b indicate whether the prefix has passed each changed station, and delta_a=u-Y_a, delta_b=v-Y_b. Its new load is exactly L-sigma_a*delta_a-sigma_b*delta_b. Thus all prefix inequalities are equivalent to integer bands

`max(L-Q_k) <= sigma_a*delta_a + sigma_b*delta_b <= min(L)`

over prefixes with the same nonzero indicator pair. Groups 10, 01 and 11 suffice (at most two are nonempty when each station has one fixed owner). Empty groups impose no restriction; 00 prefixes retain the verified baseline loads. Intersect these bands with inventory bounds. For each delta_a, the feasible delta_b values form a complete integer interval after imposing the 01 and 11 bands and checking the 10 band. Only points proved load-infeasible may be omitted this way. Record each pair's bands, capacity rectangle count, surviving interval/count and evaluated point receipts, so complete coverage and pruning are reconstructible.

The derivation is exact integer algebra. It covers same/different vehicles and sign changes. Deleting a zero operation removes a duplicate load prefix and preserves equivalence.
